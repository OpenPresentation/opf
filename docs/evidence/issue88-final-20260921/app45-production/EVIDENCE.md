This directory retains the original bounded report, logs, source/runtime and
deployment receipts and smaller diagnostic observations. Original failure frames
remain in the local capture and are identified by the retained checksums.
`SHA256SUMS` inventories the complete original local evidence, including trace
archives, screenshots and large decoded DOM files that are not all copied here.
The original report and checksum identities are unchanged. Its `/private/tmp/`
paths identify the local capture; they are not portable download links.

The separately prefixed diagnostic summary/checksums identify the one
instrumented comparison run. That passing run does not supersede 14/18 production
acceptance or explain the missing Author popup. Later follow-up acceptance must
retain its own exact source/deployment binding.

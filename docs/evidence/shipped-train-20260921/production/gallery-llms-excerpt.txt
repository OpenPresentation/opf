# PPTX.gallery

## OPF specification and editor
- /spec — Search every presentation and catalog property definition.
- /api/opf-spec.json — Full schemas and property index with SHA-256 digest.
- /editor — Visual editor and all-properties inspector. Editing coverage does not imply complete rendering fidelity.
- Color scheme pages teach ColorRef: content may use scheme slot/role names and, on OPF 0.11+, document `variables` with `var:<id>` in runs and styled table cells. Canonical prose: https://www.openpresentation.org/docs/reference/format-card
- Portable multi-file decks: `opf bundle` (ships with `@openpresentation/opf` 0.11+). Gallery editor bundle upgrades when npm pins move past 0.10.1.

## Dynamic composition
- [Composition rules and complete examples](https://www.pptx.gallery/api/composition.json): auto, grid, row, column, weights, and overflow diagnostics. Layout detail pages include an interactive text preview.
- [Pagination rules and a complete before/after example](https://www.pptx.gallery/api/pagination.json): preserve text and groups while producing ordinary OPF slides.

> Public reference catalog for OPF design choices, built for AI agents, developers, designers, and presenters.

## OPF Registry

The canonical registry index is [GET /registry.json](https://www.pptx.gallery/registry.json). It lists every registry item with `name`, `type`, `registry_url`, `dependencies`, and `opf_version`.

- opf_version: 1.0
- Descriptor pattern: `/registry/{category}/{slug}.json`
- Layouts: category `layouts`, 485 descriptors
- Color Schemes: category `color-schemes`, 14 descriptors
- Font Schemes: category `font-schemes`, 89 descriptors
- Backgrounds: category `backgrounds`, 6 descriptors
- Narratives: category `narratives`, 10 descriptors
- Charts: category `charts`, 76 descriptors
- Themes: category `themes`, 4 descriptors

## Why not infer owned process identity

[Microsoft documents PowerPoint Quit](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/office/vba/api/powerpoint.application.quit) without an OS process-exit completion primitive. Its older [COM automation guidance](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/previous-versions/office/troubleshoot/office-developer/use-visual-c-automate-run-program-instance) describes PowerPoint as a shared MultiUse server; a newly seen solePID cannot alone exclude concurrent activation or prove the COM object's ownership. The maintained bounded global-zero observation avoids that unsupported inference.

No Application.HWND/window assumption is required. The current Open is windowless. [Microsoft's window-based accessibility binding](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/api/oleacc/nf-oleacc-accessibleobjectfromwindow) requires a real document window and supplies no substitute for this contract. [A PID can be reused while retained handles remain bound to the original process](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/procthread/process-handles-and-identifiers), but that fact does not establish which process the COM client owns. No owned-handle machinery or blanket RCW-release loop is proposed here.

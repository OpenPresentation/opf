LH-112, LH-101, LH-53, LH-61, LH-15 and LH-57 (layout harvest; output-changing): metrics at display size beside other kinds, the trend arrow and delta on one line, a KPI column beside a chart, a band in a wide short cell, and the value in the text color on dark.

- **Display size beside other blocks (LH-112, core).** One or two metrics that share the body with one or two blocks of other kinds are drawn at display size: one metric's value starts at 120 reference pixels and grows to 160 while its line height stays within 40% of the body and its one line within 90% of its cell (two metrics: 90 to 120; new `layoutMetric` option `display`, `METRIC_DISPLAY_SIZES`), start-aligned with its column unless the slide or the deck sets `contentAlignment`. A metric alone or a grid of metrics alone stays centered. Both are guarded rules of the one size guard (`metric-start`, `metric-display`, the display size tried last); a value at display size is compared at its 76 px role size, its headroom above that being free for an arrangement to spend.
- **Lone metric (LH-101, core).** A lone metric grows up to nine type-scale steps while it fills at most 80% of its cell, its value up to 232 px (was 152 px and 60%): b01 s49 draws its value at 232 px (was 116), b04 s38 at 232 (was 133).
- **Trend arrow on the delta line (LH-53, core, render, PPTX).** The trend word ("up", "down", "flat") is no longer drawn: the arrow sits before the delta on one line ("↑ +7 pts"), or before the label (then the description) without a delta, at the reading start (the right of a right-to-left metric), and the word stays the arrow's text alternative ("Trend: up") and the part's source (`MetricLayout.trendBox`; the trend part is `visible: false`). The English word no longer shows on Arabic slides. A unit beside its value is drawn at 60% of the value's size on its baseline in the value's color (`METRIC_UNIT_SHARE`), so "18 %" reads as one figure. A metric with less reduced metadata is kept on a near tie, so its label keeps its size and the value gives way. PPTX import accepts both the new metric groups and exports that drew the trend word.
- **KPI column beside a chart (LH-61, core).** One or two metrics stacked over one chart (and at most one table), as the dashboard layout draws them, are composed again with the metrics in a column of about a third of the width and the chart in a full-height track beside it (`recomposed.kpis`, `track`), when the plot is at least as tall and the slide reads as well. A table that would be smaller in the column keeps the layout's arrangement (m37).
- **Band (LH-15, core).** A metric at display size in a cell at least three times as wide as it is tall draws as a band: the value at the start, its label, description and delta in a column beside it (`arrangement: "band"`, new `layoutMetric` option `band`).
- **Value color on dark (LH-57, core, render, PPTX).** On a dark slide or card a value whose accent is under 4.5:1 against it takes the light text color (`metricValueColor(color, background, text)`), not a lightened accent.
- Across the 844 LH-27 corpus slides no slide gains a finding; one slide trades its metric labels 17 to 16 px to remove all four of its findings (b11 s27); 20 slides' smallest body size goes up; 205 metric values on 96 slides grow and none shrinks. Of the 360 composition goldens, 15 move (metrics, scorecard 1, dashboard 2 to 4), none gains a finding and no item is smaller.

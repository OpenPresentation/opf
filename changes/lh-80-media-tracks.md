LH-80, LH-49 and LH-66 (layout harvest; output-changing for automatic slides with one picture or one chart among other blocks, for automatic grids of long paragraphs, and for layouts that leave a chart's plot a few pixels tall): pictures and charts get full-height tracks, and text keeps a readable measure. In automatic composition a slide's one picture block among other blocks takes a full-height track at the slide edge, as an automatic placement (`item.image.placement.automatic`), cover-cropped with the LH-84 portrait focus and bled: the start side when it is the first block, else the end side (the top or bottom of a portrait canvas), at 50, 45, 40 or 35% of the slide, never one whose crop keeps less than half of the picture. The headings and the other blocks compose beside it in the slide's own mode. A slide's one chart whose plot is too short (under the larger of 120 reference pixels and 22% of the body, or more than three times as wide as tall; new `chartPlotBox`) takes the filling track, 60, 55 or 50% of the width against a column of the other blocks, as `design.chartPrimary` splits it. A track is kept only when the slide reads as well: no more overflow or split words, no paragraph on a shorter measure, and no block's body size smaller (a slide that overflows may trade size to overflow less, never at the floor); `geometry.track` reports it. A block's own `fit: "contain"` (or a frame shape, a placement, a second picture) keeps the picture whole in the flow, as before. A layout that leaves a chart's plot shorter than its minimum is composed as the automatic grid when that draws it taller and reads as well (`recomposed.chart`; chart-beside with further charts in its notes becomes charts above their notes). A paragraph of four lines or more on a measure under 28 average characters (`MIN_MEASURE_CHARS`) adds 20 to an automatic grid candidate's score (the new `narrowMeasure` penalty), and a `grid` with no column count takes fewer columns until its paragraphs keep the measure; the measured grid is kept only when no block is smaller than without the rule. Per slide and deterministic, so the preview and the PPTX export, which both read the geometry from core, agree. Of the 360 composition goldens, 4 moved (chart-beside 3 and 6 long recompose for their charts; hero 5 and 6 long recompose as a 2 x 2 grid of paragraphs), none gained a finding; of the 844 LH-27 corpus slides, 52 moved, none gained a finding and 5 have fewer.

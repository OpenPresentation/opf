LH-114, LH-06 (widened), LH-121, LH-122 (output-changing for slides with a short quote beside data, a takeaway after two or more charts or tables, a timeline with event status, and the `timeline` layout): a quote of 140 characters or fewer that shares the slide's root `blocks` with a table, a list of three or more entries, or two or more other blocks is drawn as a full-width pull line, the last row when it is the last block and the first row under the headings when it is the first block of an automatic slide: an 80 x 6 px accent rule in the primary color, the quote at the lead size in at most two lines, and one caption line "— Name, Role" (`layoutQuote` option `pull`; `QuoteLayout.rule`; `QUOTE_PULL_RULE`, `QUOTE_PULL_PREFIX`, `QUOTE_PULL_ROLE_SEPARATOR`, `QUOTE_PULL_LINES`, `QUOTE_PULL_MAX_CHARACTERS` on `/composition`). A quote beside one compact block keeps its LH-78 card. The preview draws the rule; the PPTX export writes it as a tagged native rectangle that import consumes, and the pull line's caption imports back as `attribution` and `role`. A last text of up to 120 characters right after two or more charts or tables is LH-06's foot band (`EMPHASIS_TAKEAWAY_MAX_CHARACTERS`). A timeline whose events carry `status` draws its connector in the primary color up to the current event (`TimelineLayout.progress`) and, in an English deck, a status word (Done, Current, Planned) under each label (`TimelineLayout.statusLabels`, `TIMELINE_STATUS_WORDS`, `layoutTimeline` option `statusWords`); both engines draw them, the PPTX export as a tagged line and tagged text shapes that import consumes. The pull line and the status words are held to the size guard (`guardedCompose`): no other block reads smaller, and a timeline that would shrink its labels draws no words. Region binding keeps source order across equally ranked regions (`bindRegions`), and the `timeline` layout has a `lead` region (text, auto height) between the title and the timeline, so a text authored before the timeline is drawn under the title and one after it stays a note; an empty `lead` takes no height.

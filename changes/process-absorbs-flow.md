Breaking (0.35 layout simplification, step 4; drawing changes): the `process` layout absorbs `flow`, and `vertical-flow` is renamed `vertical-process`; the gallery has 39 layouts. A process list is drawn as Basic Process (the owner's decision for 0.35): a `process` slide whose only body content is one unnumbered list with two or more first-level entries and no icons, of any length and any count, now draws PowerPoint's Basic Process (boxes joined by arrows), the drawing the `flow` layout had, in the region's whole cell. Each first-level entry is a box; its description sits under it in the box at a description's size (0.82 of the entry's) and the entries nested under it under that, centered. The text is never smaller than the list's steps or list would be: the boxes wrap their text over as many lines as they need and snake into as many rows as the list needs (one box a row on a tall slide), at the largest size that holds them. The PPTX export writes it as live SmartArt where PowerPoint lays it out as the preview does (PowerPoint's one row of short entries, or OPF's rows of 6 to 8 entries within SH-19's domain), else as native shapes with `pptx/smartart-unavailable` and the reason (more than 8 entries in OPF's rows, an entry over two lines, text over 7.5% of the frame, or a description or nested entries, which OPF does not write in a SmartArt node). These `process` slides draw differently: a lone list of 3 to 6 one- or two-word entries drew a row of chips joined by arrows, other unnumbered lists numbered steps beside badges or a plain list (where badges would have cost a size); all now draw Basic Process at the same or a larger size (for example 37 px chips become 48 px boxes, four entries of four to six words at 37 px become boxes at 46.67 px, nine one-word entries in a 32 px list become boxes at 48 px, and three steps with descriptions at 37 and 30.33 px become boxes at 48 and 39.36 px). A numbered list keeps its numbered steps (Basic Process draws no numbers), blocks side by side keep their steps and an icon list keeps its chips; a list that no Basic Process holds at its size stays a list (`opf/arrangement-adapted`). `vertical-process` draws exactly as `vertical-flow` did. The rule lives in the `steps` arrangement, not in a layout id: any `steps` region draws its unnumbered lone list this way, and the chips are gone (`SlideComposition.steps[].form` is `row`, `column` or `tiles`; `STEP_FLOW_MIN`, `STEP_FLOW_MAX` and `STEP_FLOW_CHARACTERS` are removed and `STEP_PROCESS_MIN` (2) is the fewest first-level entries of such a list). A slide with `layout: "flow"` or `layout: "vertical-flow"` (or the `default:` forms) resolves nowhere and composes automatically: `opf/unresolved-reference` says OPF 0.35 removed the layout, names its replacement and offers the safe fix `rename-layout`, which writes `process` or `vertical-process`; core adds `renamedLayout(kind, reference)`. PPTX import puts PowerPoint's Basic Process and Vertical Process SmartArt on `process` and `vertical-process`, and a 0.34 file's `flow` and `vertical-flow` slides (stored layout or slide-layout tag) import on the new ids (a stored layout reports `layout-renamed`). The PPTX export writes one slide layout fewer (no `flow`) and names the vertical one "Vertical process". The gallery's `process` description says that a list draws as boxes and a numbered one as steps; the layout sampler drops its `flow` slide (later slides move up one) and the examples, qualification decks, skills and docs use the new ids. `docs/migrations/0.35.md` lists each change.

Schema removal: `layout.schema.json:$defs.Region.properties.arrangement` loses the values `flow` and `vertical-flow` (use `process` and `vertical-process`).

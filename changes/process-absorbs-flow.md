Breaking (0.35 layout simplification, step 4; drawing changes): the `process` layout absorbs `flow`, and `vertical-flow` is renamed `vertical-process`; the gallery has 39 layouts. A `process` slide whose only body content is one list of 2 to 8 entries of one level, each of one or two words and at most 24 characters, without numbering, descriptions or icons, now draws PowerPoint's Basic Process (boxes joined by arrows, live SmartArt in the PPTX export where PowerPoint lays it out as the preview does), the drawing the `flow` layout had, in the region's whole cell, at the size its steps would have or larger; where Basic Process cannot hold the entries at that size the list keeps its steps. These `process` slides draw differently: a lone list of 3 to 6 such entries drew a row of chips joined by arrows (a plain list where the chips did not fit one line), and one of 2, 7 or 8 entries drew numbered steps beside badges or a plain list (where badges would have cost a size); all now draw Basic Process, at the same or a larger size (for example 37 px chips become 48 px boxes, and a plain list of six or eight two-word steps at 32 px becomes boxes at 48 or 42.7 px). Longer lists, longer entries, lists beside other blocks and numbered steps with descriptions draw as before, and `vertical-process` draws exactly as `vertical-flow` did. The rule lives in the `steps` arrangement, not in a layout id: any `steps` region draws its short lone list this way, and the chips are gone (`SlideComposition.steps[].form` is `row`, `column` or `tiles`; `STEP_FLOW_MIN`, `STEP_FLOW_MAX` and `STEP_FLOW_CHARACTERS` become `STEP_PROCESS_MIN` 2, `STEP_PROCESS_MAX` 8 and `STEP_PROCESS_CHARACTERS` 24). A slide with `layout: "flow"` or `layout: "vertical-flow"` (or the `default:` forms) resolves nowhere and composes automatically: `opf/unresolved-reference` says OPF 0.35 removed the layout, names its replacement and offers the safe fix `rename-layout`, which writes `process` or `vertical-process`; core adds `renamedLayout(kind, reference)`. PPTX import puts PowerPoint's Basic Process and Vertical Process SmartArt on `process` and `vertical-process`, and a 0.34 file's `flow` and `vertical-flow` slides (stored layout or slide-layout tag) import on the new ids (a stored layout reports `layout-renamed`). The PPTX export writes one slide layout fewer (no `flow`) and names the vertical one "Vertical process". The gallery's `process` description says what a short list draws; the layout sampler drops its `flow` slide (later slides move up one) and the examples, qualification decks, skills and docs use the new ids. `docs/migrations/0.35.md` lists each change.

Schema removal: `layout.schema.json:$defs.Region.properties.arrangement` loses the values `flow` and `vertical-flow` (use `process` and `vertical-process`).

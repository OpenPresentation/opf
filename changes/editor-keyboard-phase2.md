Source editor, keyboard and IntelliSense parity phase 2 (YAML and Markdown get the quality JSON has): completion of keys, values, enums, catalog ids, templates and snippets in YAML (block and flow style, after `key: `, after a list dash) and in Markdown (the front matter and the YAML of `opf-slide` and `opf-block` fences; slide and block options comments; fence kinds, chart types and attributes; metric fences; inline span attributes; `Note:` and slide snippets), Tab and Enter accept, hover documentation in both, and the same snippets written as YAML and as Markdown (with the `---` separator). Enter and indentation follow the text: one level in after `key:`, aligned under the key after `- key: value`, the next `- ` after a list item, an empty marker ends the list; Markdown continues `-`, `*`, `+` and `1.` items and `>` quotes, Tab nests a list item and Backspace takes a marker away. `[`, `{` and quotes pair in YAML, `(`, `[`, `{` and backticks in Markdown, `*`, `_`, `~` and `"` wrap a selection; Cmd/Ctrl+/ toggles `# ` in YAML and the front matter and `<!-- -->` in Markdown, and Cmd/Ctrl+Alt+[ folds a Markdown slide, fence or front matter. Squiggles, F8, quick fixes (`Change to`, `Rename to`, `Remove property`, `Add`) and the `Ln n, Col m` of the problems panel are at the right place for YAML and Markdown findings (core's `locate`), and `focusPath` finds the line the same way. "Canonicalize with OPF" is its own button in YAML and Markdown (`pane.canonicalize()`): the old Format rewrite from the document, which asks first and names how many comments it drops; Format keeps comments. Keyboard cases in Chromium and WebKit, unit tests and the 300-slide budgets for YAML and Markdown.

LH-189 (output-changing for display text): a title or subtitle, a slide's message text, a one-message quote's body and a growing lone metric's value set at twice the body size or more, in Latin, Greek or Cyrillic text or ASCII figures, take -1.5% tracking when they fit on one line, and take a 1.0 em line pitch with that tracking only when they take two lines and the tighter pitch lets them reach a larger size (composed both ways, the tighter kept only when it is larger and nothing is smaller); three lines or more keep the 1.22 em pitch and no tracking, and a metric label or quote attribution set in capitals takes the eyebrow's +8% tracking (`linePitch`, `displayFrom`, `DISPLAY_TRACKING`, `displayTracks`, `capitalsLabel`; `fitText` and `RichTextOptions` take `displayFrom`, and `RichTextOptions` takes `displayPitch`). Measurement, the preview and the PPTX export read the same fit and style, so the export writes each line where core placed it and the tracking as `spc`; headings over a measured background picture keep the body pitch and spacing.

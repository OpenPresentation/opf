Breaking (0.35 layout simplification, step 5; the built-in layouts draw as before): one vocabulary for "row or column". A layout region's `flow` takes the words of `composition.mode`: `auto`, `grid`, `row` and `column`. `row` is new: the region's blocks side by side in one row, one cell each at equal widths, kept as written (no paragraph columns, lead block, list grid or regrouping), as `design.contentDirection: "horizontal"` made a region before. `flow: "none"` is removed: a region of one block says `max: 1`, and a region of `max: 1` keeps every rule `none` had (its block fills it; on a layout with no overflow region, blocks no region had room for join it, up to 6, with no finding, and flow by its `flow`; side-by-side single-block regions of one role share their joined blocks; it is never the default `overflowRegion` nor the region a slide's own `composition` arranges); a custom record's `max: 1` on a flowing region now means the same. `design.contentDirection` and `design.chartPrimary` are removed from the deck, slide, theme and layout `design`, with no alias: composition no longer reads them (an automatic slide says its arrangement with `composition.mode`, a chart beside its supporting blocks is the `chart-beside` layout, mirrored for the end side, and a short chart still takes LH-49's track), the `arrangement-adapted` finding at `design.chartPrimary` is gone, and `DESIGN_HINT_KEYS`, `THEME_DESIGN_KEYS` and `DesignHints` drop them. Validation reports a document or record that still uses them at the key (`opf/schema`, or `opf/catalog-record` for an embedded record) with what replaced it and a safe fix: `remove:contentDirection` and `remove:chartPrimary` delete the key, and `use-max-1` turns a region's `flow: "none"` into `max: 1`. The gallery records that used `none` (23 layouts) say `max: 1`, and `process` says `flow: "row"` instead of `design.contentDirection: "horizontal"`; each draws as before. The gallery example generator no longer writes `contentDirection` and `chartPrimary` (a pptx.gallery dimension), and its 100 decks are regenerated; they draw exactly as before (720 slides: their automatic slides use promoted regions, and the regions of their layout slides already arranged their blocks the way the keys asked). `layoutStructure` writes `body (auto, max 1: …)` for a single-block region. The editor drops the Content direction and Primary chart position controls and design options, and its layout inspector offers the four flows. PPTX export no longer stores the two keys, and import of a 0.34 file does not restore them. The `drawing` declaration is for the gallery stability check, which draws the 0.34 records with the 0.35 engine: there a 0.34 `flow: "none"` no longer reads (the region is a flowing region with room for 6, so the stability fixture's other blocks bind differently), so `timeline`, `metrics`, `chart`, `table` and `hero` draw differently, while the 0.35 records draw as the 0.34 records did (the render template goldens: 702 slides, no PNG or SVG change). `docs/migrations/0.35.md` lists every old key and value.

Schema removal: `layout.schema.json:$defs.Region.properties.flow` loses the value `none` (use `max: 1`).

Two option regions side by side in a layout (two equal columns of single-block regions, each holding one paragraph or list, on a slide without cards, such as `comparison`) whose texts differ 2.5 times or more in length split the row in favor of the longer one, up to 3:1, on its own side: a long Option B beside a short Option A keeps the body size, both options start on one line, and the recommendation keeps its banner, where two equal halves set the long option smaller and dropped the banner.

FA-14 (additive schema and core API; lockstep with the renderer, PPTX and editor): chart emphasis. `chart.highlight: { series?: string[], categories?: string[] }` names the series (data columns) and categories (row labels) that carry the message; the engines draw those marks in the deck primary color and mute every other mark with a neutral derived from the theme. There is no color field. A mark is highlighted when its series OR its category is named; a chart without `highlight` keeps its colors byte for byte. Contract: [docs/chart-options.md](docs/chart-options.md).
    - **Types:** column, bar and line charts take series and categories (a line's category highlight marks its points), area, scatter and radar charts series, pie and doughnut charts categories (slices); the histogram, pareto, waterfall, funnel, treemap, box-and-whisker and map constructs drop the option with a `chart-option-adapted` diagnostic (option `highlight.series` or `highlight.categories`), as does a part a type cannot highlight.
    - **Validation:** a series or category the data does not have is the `opf/chart-highlight-unknown-name` error (category `format`, found by `resolveChartData`, like `opf/chart-mapping-unknown-column`); a highlighted column that is not plotted as a series is the `opf/chart-highlight-adapted` warning (category `content`). The schema requires at least one name and rejects duplicates.
    - **Core API** (`@openpresentation/opf/composition`): `chartHighlightMarks(highlight, resolvedData)` (which series and rows are named, shared by the engines), `chartHighlightColors(surface, primary, text)` (the accent, kept at 3:1, and the muted neutral, the surface mixed 30% toward the text color and never under 1.6:1), the constants `CHART_HIGHLIGHT_MUTED_MIX` and `CHART_HIGHLIGHT_MUTED_MIN_CONTRAST`, `ChartOptionSupport.highlight` (`chartOptionSupport`), `ResolvedChartOptions.highlight` (`resolveChartOptions`) and the types `ResolvedChartHighlight` and `ChartHighlightMarks`. A highlight never makes `ResolvedChartOptions.active` true: it changes colors, not geometry.
    - **Example:** `examples/technical/chart-highlight.opf.json` (a highlighted series, a highlighted bar and a highlighted pie) is the 127th bundled example deck (805 to 808 golden slides). `scripts/fixtures/opf-examples-png.example-decks.sha256.json`, the coordinated golden, gains its three slides and the new corpus digest; the other 805 hashes are unchanged. The Markdown dialect has no form for chart options, so such a chart is kept in an embedded block (lossless).

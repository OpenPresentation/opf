LH-165, LH-162 (output-changing for short lists with icons, groups with icons and grids of short paragraphs): icon cards and text tiles. A slide whose whole body is one list of 2 to 8 first-level entries, each with an `icon`, a short label (at most 48 characters and 7 words) and at most a short description, draws as a grid of equal cards that take the body's height (or the layout region's): two to four a row, balanced, the short last row centered (`cardGridColumns`), in the list's order from the start edge. Each card is the theme's card surface (the card neutral and border LH-137's check-mark tiles use) with the icon's chip at the top start (solid in the primary), the label bold and the description under it; the chip stands above the label, beside the label and its description, or beside the label with the description under both, whichever lets the text be largest. A numbered list and an agenda's list (`agenda-split`) keep their numbers as numerals at the top end of each card; a numbered list's numerals carry the list numbers, so a PPTX import restores `numbering`. A set of 2 to 8 groups that is the whole body, each with an `icon`, a short label and an optional description paragraph, draws the same cards with the label bold instead of an eyebrow. 4 to 8 short paragraphs (90 characters at most) at the root of an automatic slide draw as tiles in the same grid: one card each, a small accent dot at the start of the text's first line, the text in the middle of its tile on at most two lines. The text of both starts at the size it has and grows by type-scale steps up to LH-05's 56 px (one size on every card, a description at 0.82 of its label) while the cards hold it and either the tallest card's text takes at most 80% of its card (icon cards) or the text covers at most 60% of the body; a card label keeps the lines it has at the starting size (three words or more may take two). A layout's lone list with icons (an `agenda-split` agenda) also tries its cards across the whole body under the heading (the slide recomposed as the automatic grid, `recomposed.cards`), kept when its text reads at least as large; the agenda keeps its numbers. Both are new guarded rules (`icon-cards`, `text-tiles`) tried on top of the rules the guard keeps, and kept only when no block is smaller than without them, also against the settled slide without them; otherwise the slide draws as before. Both engines draw the cards as native shapes (LH-137's tile chips, numerals and badges in the steps layer, LH-14's icons), and the text as measured lines.

SH-08: SmartArt that PowerPoint users made imports into OPF with its text. Import reads the data model's outline (the entries in order, nested entries, an organization chart's assistants, a name-and-title box as the entry's description, run formatting, line breaks), and the layout decides the form: the pyramid, matrix, cycle and chevron families and Basic Process and Vertical Process become a list on the `pyramid`, `matrix`, `cycle`, `chevrons`, `flow` or `vertical-flow` layout, the list and process families a list on `list` or `process`, hierarchies and organization charts a Mermaid flowchart (assistants dotted, a connection's text as the edge label), and every other layout (Venn, radial, target...) its drawing as overlay shapes through the shape importer, the outline as the first shape's alt text. Nested entries keep their `level` (they were dropped before, with `smartart-flattened`, which is removed). What a form does not keep is reported: `smartart-text-dropped`, `smartart-picture-dropped`, `smartart-formatting-dropped`; `smartart-adapted` (info) names the form chosen. Each point is imported once, under the first parent that reaches it (`smartart-connection-dropped` counts the further connections), and the outline is read as a tree at most 64 points deep and 10,000 entries long; past that the drawing, or the entries as a flat list, is imported (`smartart-limited`). A SmartArt whose data model or drawing cannot be read imports as an ordinary graphic frame (`smartart-unreadable`) and never fails the import. A SmartArt that imported as an unknown "PowerPoint object" before now imports as content. An Inverted Pyramid imports as a list in order (OPF's pyramid has no inverted form). Core: front overlay shapes whose bounds cover at least a fifth of the slide (`overlaysDrawBody`, `OVERLAY_BODY_SHARE`) are the slide's body, so a slide of a title and a drawing of overlay shapes puts its title on top instead of centering it over the shapes as a cover; a small mark on a cover changes nothing.

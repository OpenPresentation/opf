SH-08: SmartArt that PowerPoint users made imports into OPF with its text. The data model outline is read (entries, nested entries with their `level`, organization chart assistants, name-and-title boxes, run formatting) and the layout decides the form: pyramid, matrix, cycle, chevron, Basic Process and Vertical Process become a list on the `pyramid`, `matrix`, `cycle`, `chevrons`, `flow` or `vertical-flow` layout; list and process families a list on `list` or `process`; hierarchies and organization charts a Mermaid flowchart; every other layout (Venn, radial, target...) overlay shapes, with the outline as the first shape alt text. An Inverted Pyramid imports as a list in order. What a form does not keep is reported (`smartart-text-dropped`, `smartart-picture-dropped`, `smartart-formatting-dropped`, `smartart-connection-dropped`, `smartart-limited`; `smartart-adapted` names the form chosen); `smartart-flattened` is removed. A SmartArt that cannot be read imports as a graphic frame (`smartart-unreadable`) and never fails the import. Core: front overlay shapes covering at least a fifth of the slide (`overlaysDrawBody`, `OVERLAY_BODY_SHARE`) are the slide body, so a title over a drawing of shapes is placed on top instead of centered as a cover.

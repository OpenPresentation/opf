LH-90, LH-91 (output-changing for overfull slides of sibling blocks, and for recomposed slides under `bodyText: "native"`): an overfull slide continues on a new slide instead of drawing overlapping bands, and a slide recomposed at the readability floor keeps native bodies.
    - LH-90: `continueOverfull(presentation, options)` (core, with `blockUnits(slide)`) continues a slide whose body is two or more units of one kind (eight timelines, five tables; a unit is a block of the first block's kind with the blocks that follow it, so a timeline keeps its note) and that overflows at its own readability floor on further slides of the same layout. It is `paginateSlide` with the new `wholeBlocks: true`: breaks only between whole units, the fewest pages that fit with the units spread evenly, headings repeated, notes on the first page, no region pins on later pages, the slide's composition kept as authored. A slide that fits, a unit that does not fit alone, a strict composition (`overflow: "error"`, which still fails) and an unsettled `{{deck.slideCount}}` keep the slide as written. Nothing is dropped.
    - The Node export (`convert`, `opf convert`) applies it by default before drawing (`paginate: "blocks"`), so SVG, PNG, PDF and PPTX draw the same pages, and reports each slide as `render/slide-continued` or `pptx/slide-continued` (a warning) and in the new report field `continued`. `paginate: true` (`--paginate`) still splits every overflowing slide; `paginate: false` (new `--keep-slides`) draws every slide as written. `toSvg`, `toPptx`, the player and the editor still draw the deck they are given.
    - LH-91: under `bodyText: "native"` a slide recomposed as the automatic grid (LH-83) no longer falls back to measured shapes (`native-body-fallback`): its first text or list binds to the Body placeholder of a variant of its layout (`<layout> (automatic grid)`) at the recomposed body's box, the others are native text boxes, and import still reads the slide's layout from the layout tag.

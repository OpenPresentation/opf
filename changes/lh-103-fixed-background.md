LH-103 (output-changing for decks with a fixed background): a fixed background the author set beats a gallery layout's `design.variant`. When the deck or the theme draws a hex color, a solid whose color is a `var:` reference, a gradient, a pattern or a picture, the section, statement, quote and closing slides (and any layout that names a variant) keep that background, and their text follows its `tone` or luminance as on any other slide; the preview draws the deck's picture or color and the PPTX export writes no `clrMapOvr` or scheme background for them. A scheme-based deck background (none, a theme slot, a solid whose color is a scheme slot or role name such as `primary`, or a deck `variant`) still takes the layout's variant on top, and the slide's own `variant` or `background` still wins. A deck with both a fixed background and a `variant` keeps the background; a deck `variant` hides the theme's background, so the layout's variant applies there. New on `/composition`: `isFixedBackground(definition)` and `deckBackgroundIsFixed(deckDesign, themeBackground)`; `layoutVariantApplies(slideDesign, layout, deckDesign?, themeBackground?)` takes the deck design and the theme background.

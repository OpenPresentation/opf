SH-17: three more diagram layouts, `chevrons` (a row of chevrons), `flow` (boxes joined by arrows in a row) and `vertical-flow` (the same as a column beside the title), each a region's new `arrangement` value of the same name for one list of 1 to 8 stages or steps in order. Where it holds the entries they draw PowerPoint's own geometry for its Basic Chevron Process, Basic Process and Vertical Process SmartArt, measured through COM and fitted in core (`SMART_ART_SCALES`: PowerPoint sizes a node by the last step of a binary search, a share of the frame that depends only on the node count, so core matches every measured frame within 0.0001 pt), and the PPTX export writes them as live SmartArt (`chevron1`, `process1`, `process2`) with SH-16's fallback picture, inside the domain the COM sweep passed: Latin text, 1 to 6 chevrons or boxes in a row at 1.1 to 2.3 : 1, 1 to 4 boxes in a column at 0.55 to 1.2 : 1, at most two lines an entry, no entry filling more than 95% of its node's height, and the text covering at most 8% of the frame. Elsewhere OPF draws its own design, as native shapes in both engines, in PowerPoint's proportions (its arrows, rooms and least node heights): the text at the largest size every node holds (up to 1.5 times the role size, never below it), each node as tall as that text needs, centered; in one to four rows (a vertical flow: columns) when that holds the text larger, every row spanning the region, a flow's rows snaking with a down arrow from one row's end to the next (a vertical flow's columns with a right arrow), a row of chevrons breaking after a point; a right-to-left deck mirrors it. The arrows are the primary lightened 40%.

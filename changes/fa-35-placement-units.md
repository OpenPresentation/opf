Give anchored overlay sizes one unit per key: `width` and `height` are fractions of their target, while `widthPx` and `heightPx` are reference pixels. To update a 0.33 deck, rename an anchored `width` or `height` that holds pixels to `widthPx` or `heightPx` (`"at": { "width": 160 }` becomes `"at": { "widthPx": 160 }`); a pixel value above 8 is now a schema error that asks `Did you mean "widthPx"?` and carries the rename as a patch, and a value above 4 and up to 8 still validates as a fraction with the warning `opf/shape-anchor-size-unit`, which offers the same rename. See the [0.34 migration](../docs/migrations/0.34.md). Breaking for a deck with an overlay shape larger than eight slides: `OverlayShape.width` and `height` and `OverlayAnchor.width` and `height` have a maximum of 8 (8 slides, or 8 times the target), and a larger value is a schema error. The editor's frame changes and handles stop at 8, and PPTX import limits a native shape larger than 8 slides to 8 and reports it (`shape-property-dropped`, "shape larger than eight slides"), keeping its signed position. Editor changes are undoable and refuse duplicate units; native provenance distinguishes legacy pixel anchors from current fractional anchors.

LH-03 and LH-81 (output-changing for column, bar, line, area and combo charts): charts carry their own caption and legend, and small charts label their values.
    - **Small charts (LH-03).** A clustered column, bar or line chart of one or two series with eight or fewer points per series, whose labels fit beside each other, labels every mark with its value (outside the end of a column or bar, above a line point), drops the value axis, its gridlines and the grey chart panel, and, with one column or bar series, draws its last point in the series colour and the others in a quieter tint of it (core `chartEmphasisColors`): the tint keeps 3:1 against the backdrop (WCAG 1.4.11) and 15 L* from the series colour, and where the backdrop leaves no room for both every point keeps the series colour. In PPTX the value axis is deleted (`c:delete val="1"`), the chart area has no fill and each quieter point is a `c:dPt`.
    - **Header row (LH-81).** A column, bar, line, area or combo chart draws a caption above the plot when it adds to the legend (one series: "Count by stage"; several series: only from a value axis title, "Revenue ($M) by quarter"; the category in sentence case, acronyms such as "Q1" and "FY27" kept; the series part alone outside English decks) and, for two to four series, its legend in that row or the next instead of at the side, so the plot keeps the chart's full width. In PPTX the caption is the chart title, and the title, the top legend and the plot area (`layoutTarget outer`) carry core's positions as `c:manualLayout`.
    - Composition decides both once per chart item: `ComposedChart.layer` (core `chartLayer`, `chartHeader`, `chartHeaderCaption`, `smallChart`, `chartLayerDataLabels`, on `/composition`), and the composed value axis is sized for the plot below the header. An explicit option wins: `dataLabels: false` keeps a small chart's value axis and draws no labels, a value axis title keeps the axis, `highlight` and `colors` keep their colours, a `legend` option keeps that legend and draws no header row, and a payload `caption` replaces the header caption. `fromPptx` does not write the default labels or the header legend back as options.

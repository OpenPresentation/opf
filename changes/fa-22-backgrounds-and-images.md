FA-22 (breaking, 0.15 spec): a full-bleed photo is a background and every content picture is an image block, with one fit vocabulary. The image background is flat: `{ type: "image", src, alt?, fit?: cover | contain | stretch | tile, focus?: { x, y }, opacity?, recolor?, overlay? }` (recolor is grayscale or duotone, as on image blocks; the overlay may be an edge band) (no `image` wrapper), and the background string shorthand also takes image sources (`asset:`, `https://`, `data:`, `./`, `../`) as a cover image; any other string that is not a theme slot or hex colour is invalid. Image payloads gain `fit` (`cover | contain | stretch`), `focus`, the treatments of 0.14's slide image (`shape`, `cornerRadius`, `border`, `opacity`, `recolor`, `overlay`, `aspectRatio`) and `placement: { edge, size, inset }`, which bleeds a top-level block to one slide edge while the headings and the other content compose in the rest (at most one per edge; a layout image placeholder may carry the same placement). One `Overlay` definition serves backgrounds and image blocks. `design.imageFit` (default `cover`) replaces `design.imageFill` (`crop` is `cover`, `fit` is `contain`; content images defaulted to `fit` before). Removed: `design.slideImage`, the layout-level `design.slideImage` opt-in and the `image-bleed` layout record (99 bundled layouts remain). New validation rules `opf/image-option-unsupported-payload` and `opf/image-placement-invalid`. `promoteImage` and `demoteImage` move an image to and from `background` and `watermark` only. The repository's examples, fixtures and layout records are rewritten; see [images](docs/image-treatments.md).

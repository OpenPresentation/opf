LH-02 (output-changing for slides with text on a picture background): an automatic scrim under text on a picture background. When text sits on a picture and the background has no `overlay`, core adds a scrim in the slide's background color behind the text (`SlideComposition.backgroundImage.scrim`): a gradient from the bottom, top or side edge the text is near, or a band across centered text, at the lowest opacity (in hundredths) that keeps the slide's text at 4.5:1 over any picture, the gray ramp from black to white. A host that can read pixels passes `pictureLuminance` (a compose option, and an option of `toSvg` and `toPptx`) and the scrim is sized for the picture under the text instead. The preview draws it as an SVG linear gradient and the PPTX export as one native rectangle with a `bg1` gradient fill whose stops carry alpha; it replaces RR-96's 40% scrim on a picture that names its `tone`, and import reads it back as no content. An overlay of the author's always wins, and the new `overlay: false` on an image background turns the scrim off (the editor's background panel has a "No automatic scrim" box for it). `opf/text-on-image` notes the scrim, and the contrast rules now read a slide's own design (its background, tone or variant), which they had dropped.

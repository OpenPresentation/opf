SH-18: cycles, written to PowerPoint as live Basic Cycle SmartArt. A layout region's `arrangement` takes `"cycle"`, and the gallery adds the `cycle` layout (33 layouts): its lone list of 1 to 8 entries draws as the steps of a loop, each a node on a ring with an arrow to the next, in the first design that holds every entry at one size, the list's role size or larger, never smaller. PowerPoint's design is its Basic Cycle (`cycle2`), measured through COM: OPF gives the SmartArt frame the cycle's own proportion (1.8 : 1 for two entries, 1.15 : 1 otherwise), at which PowerPoint's layout is exactly proportional to the frame, and reproduces its circles (`ellipse`) and turned `rightArrow` arrows from the frame's width (core `cycleFrame`, `cycleNodes`, `CYCLE_DIAMETER`, `CYCLE_RADIUS`; every circle and arrow within 0.0001 pt). Where the circles do not hold the entries OPF draws its own cycle, the larger-text of a ring of rounded rectangles filling the region and a loop of two or three rows with a node on every place of its edge (a row of fewer nodes widens them, so no place is ever empty; `cycleRing`, `cycleLoop`), with the same arrows; a cycle of more than 8 entries, or one no design holds, stays a list with `opf/arrangement-adapted`. Such a list (of a pyramid, matrix or cycle region alike) now fills the region: the largest size up to 1.5 times the role size at which the whole list fits, in the middle of the region's height, where before it kept the role size at the top of the region. The arrows are the primary lightened 40% (`arrangementPaint().arrow`), and a right-to-left deck's cycle runs counterclockwise. The PPTX export writes PowerPoint's design as live SmartArt (with SH-16's fallback picture) for Latin, left-to-right entries of at most two lines each; everything else is native shapes with `pptx/smartart-unavailable`. Import reads an OPF cycle back from its `mc:Choice`, its fallback picture alone or its native shapes, and a PowerPoint-authored Basic Cycle from its data model, never storing a layout part. See `docs/smartart.md`.

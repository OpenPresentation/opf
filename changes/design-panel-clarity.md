The Design panel (the Studio's and the Playground's design controls) shows options instead of explaining them. Helper paragraphs under controls are gone: a hint, a unit or a range is the control's tooltip and accessible description (the composition's gap and padding say "share of the container's shorter side" there), number fields show their range as a placeholder, and long labels are short ("Focus X", "Aspect ratio", "Watermark opacity", "Replace with", "Today's date"). Every content type is listed; one the selected content cannot become is disabled, with a short reason ("Metric: The first line is longer than 24 characters.") as its tooltip and accessible description instead of a bullet list. A block's structural actions are one row of named icon buttons: Group with the next block and Ungroup (which now also dissolves a selected group), and in the Studio Move up, Move down, Duplicate and Delete with their shortcuts in the tooltip; a disabled action stays focusable and names why. List levels are two icon buttons. The block's path moved from a "Text block (slides.0.blocks.0)" line to a copyable chip under Advanced, and the image and table sections no longer print their paths. Reassurance copy ("Nothing is lost", "Undo restores the previous state", "Undo brings it back") is gone from the panel, its status messages and the chart options; a status says what changed and what a change did not carry over. The Studio's arrangement is four named icon segments, the heading and part views are an Edit text and a Select block button, and a block's actions and content type come before its own sections. New design-controls options: `showPath` and `blockActions`.

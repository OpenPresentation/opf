LH-97, LH-123 and LH-126 (the editor's follow-ups of the layout variants, the trend arrow and `fit: "auto"`):
    - The color variant picker offers `accent1` to `accent6` after the four backgrounds (an accent is the slide background, with the light or dark text family that reads better on it, from core's `colorMapFor`), and selects the variant a slide's layout decides when the slide sets none: a section on its accent, a statement or quote on `surface` (light2 on a light deck, dark2 on a dark one), a closing on `brand`. The selected swatch names where it comes from ("from the layout's surface variant"); `readDesignVariant` reports it as `scope: "layout"` with `layout: { background, role? }` (the deck scope never reports a layout, which belongs to its slides); `variantSwatches` returns ten swatches; `normalizeVariant` accepts every `VARIANT_SLOTS` slot. Resolution is core's (`resolveSlideContext`), not a copy.
    - The trend arrow (LH-53 drew no trend word, so there was nothing to edit in place) is a click and keyboard target on the canvas over core's `trendBox`: it opens the trend field, the properties form with the choices up, down and flat. The selection panel shows the metric's trend as a "Trend" select (None, Up, Down, Flat). New on `/content-actions`: `METRIC_TRENDS`, `readMetricTrend`, `prepareMetricTrend` and `setMetricTrend` (one undoable step; a bare metric value becomes `{ value, trend }`).
    - The image Fit select reads what `auto` resolved to from the composed `image.fit` and `image.autoFit`: "Auto (cover)" or "Auto (contain)", and "Presentation default (Auto, cover)" when the deck or layout default is auto and the block names none. New on `/image-options`: `readEffectiveImageFit(geometry, blockPath)`. The design controls take an optional `getGeometry(slideIndex)` (the workspace, the playground and the Studio pass the slide as they draw it, with their fonts); without it the panel composes with `editor.composeSlide`.

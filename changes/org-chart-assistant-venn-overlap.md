An org chart's assistant and a Venn's overlap label. A list entry with `assistant: true` on the `org-chart` layout (a chief of staff, an executive assistant, written among its manager's reports) is drawn as PowerPoint's organization chart draws an assistant: one row below its manager, to the right of the line down to the reports, joined to that line by an elbow, with the reports a row lower. A last list entry with `overlap: true` on the `venn` layout labels where all of two to seven sets meet: an opaque pill in the slide's background color with the primary outline, over the common overlap, at the size of the set labels, moved up or down only as far as it must to clear the set labels. Both keep the list's size or grow it; a tree or Venn that cannot hold them (an assistant with reports, the root as an assistant, an assistant in a `hierarchy`, an overlap label that is not last or has fewer than two sets) keeps the complete list with `opf/arrangement-adapted`. The SVG preview and the PPTX export draw both from core's shared geometry as editable shapes (a rectangle and two line segments; a fully rounded `roundRect` under a text box), and `fromPptx` puts `assistant` and `overlap` back on their entries from the nodes' tags.

LH-110 (breaking, output-changing for charts whose title or subtitle compares with a value or names a period): chart reference lines are explicit. 0.33 drew a dashed line for a number an English title or subtitle compared the data with ("stayed above 92%") and shaded a period it named ("in 2023"); that inference is removed, and period bands have no replacement. To keep a target line, write `"referenceLines": [{"value": 0.92, "label": "Target 92%", "stroke": {"dash": "dash"}}]` on a column, bar, line, line-with-markers, area or combo chart (entries take a value in the units of the axis, an optional `label`, an optional `axis` and a `stroke` of `{ color?, width?, dash? }` or `"none"`); another chart type is the error `opf/chart-reference-unsupported-type`. Targets count in the automatic bounds of their axis; the PPTX export writes native helper line series (vertical charts with two or more categories) or line and text overlays (bars, single-category charts), and import restores verified helpers and keeps uncertain ones with a diagnostic. New examples: `explicit-chart-reference-lines`, `singleton-combo-reference-lines` and `localized-chart-references` under `examples/technical/`.

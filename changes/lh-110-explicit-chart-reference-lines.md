LH-110 (breaking, output-changing for charts whose title or subtitle compares with a value or names a period): chart reference lines are now explicit. v0.33.0 drew a dashed reference line for a number an English title or subtitle compared the data with ("stayed above 92%") and shaded the period an English title named ("in 2023"); that inference is removed, and no title, subtitle or language creates a line or a band any more. To keep a target line, write it on the chart: `"referenceLines": [{"value": 0.92, "label": "Target 92%", "stroke": {"dash": "dash"}}]` (ordered entries with a value in the axis's units, an optional verbatim endpoint label, an optional `axis` and an independent ColorRef/width/solid-dash-dot `stroke`, or `"stroke": "none"` for the label alone) on a column, bar, line, line-with-markers, area or combo chart; another type is the error `opf/chart-reference-unsupported-type`. Period bands have no replacement. Targets are included in the shared automatic bounds of their own axis; the preview draws them, and the PPTX export writes native constant helper line series for vertical charts with two or more categories and fixed line and text overlays for bars and single-category charts. Import restores verified current helpers once and keeps uncertain helpers and v0.33 inferred shapes with a diagnostic. New examples: `examples/technical/explicit-chart-reference-lines.opf.json`, `singleton-combo-reference-lines.opf.json` and `localized-chart-references.opf.json` (authored labels in seven languages).

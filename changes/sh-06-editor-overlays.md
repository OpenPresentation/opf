SH-06 part B: the editor edits overlay shapes and connectors (`slide.shapes`, SH-04). `@openpresentation/opf-editor/shape-overlays` inserts (`insertOverlayShape`), moves and resizes (`setOverlayFrame`: fractions of the slide kept on it; a shape attached to a block with `at` keeps its anchor and takes `offset`, `width` and `height`, `detachOverlay` frees it), restacks (`moveOverlayLayer`: array order), deletes (`removeOverlay`, with the connectors that end on the shape) and connects (`connectShapes`: two blocks or overlay shapes, ends without an `id` given one in the same patch; `setConnector` changes route, ends and sites) as one undoable step each. The playground's **Insert shape** places a shape **In the layout** or **On the slide**, **Connect** (and the canvas's `connect({ route, stroke })`) picks two things on the slide and joins them, a selected overlay shape shows a frame with eight resize handles and a move grip (its outline moves it, arrow keys nudge it), and the Selected shape section has Place on the slide, Stacking, Delete and, for a connector, route, start and end, connection sites, line and arrowheads. A connector or overlay is selected, not edited as text, when clicked. See docs/shapes.md.

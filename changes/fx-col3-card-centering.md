A short text beside a longer text or list on a row of cards is centered in its card instead of starting at the card's top over an empty band (COL-3). In a row of cards that hug the tallest text, a shorter text now sits in the middle of its card; in a middle-anchored row of very unequal texts (the `two-column`, `three-column`, `pillars` and `team` layouts), each text is centered in its filled card. A bottom-anchored row keeps starting very unequal texts at the card's top.

COL-2 (output-changing for a column layout whose cards hold one table and short texts): a table on a column's card grows with the texts on the cards beside it (LH-48 with TAB-1: type-scale steps up to its display size, its rows with it, the body covering at most three quarters of its height), instead of a thin band at its role size. A table that only fits its column at the readability floor (a four-column table in a third of the slide) keeps its size.

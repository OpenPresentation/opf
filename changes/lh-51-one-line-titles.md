LH-51, LH-76 (output-changing for content slides with long titles): a content slide's title steps down from 54 reference px in three equal steps (49.5, 45, 40.5) to the largest that keeps it on one line, and wraps at 54 px and balances, as before, only when it needs two lines even at 40.5. Covers, sections, statements, quotes, closings and `agenda-split` keep 54 px. A "Topic: qualifier" title (its first colon followed by a space) that still wraps, on a slide with no subtitle, draws the topic as the title on one line and the qualifier below it at the subtitle size; the document keeps the whole title, and both composed items carry the title's path and the new `ComposedItem.titleSplit` (`part`, `start`, `end`). The PPTX export puts the topic in the Title placeholder and the qualifier in a text box below it, tagged as one title group (a `split` line boundary that carries the authored colon), so import restores the whole authored title. Per slide, not per deck: a deck has at most four content title sizes. With `trace`, the preview draws each part's source ranges in the authored title. The editor edits a split title in place as the whole authored title: a press on the topic or the qualifier opens the inline input at the pressed character, the input shows the whole title (stepped down to fit) over both parts, and a commit writes the whole title.

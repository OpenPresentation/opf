Ordinary body paragraphs that exceed the reading measure now try source-aware reflow at their already-settled font sizes. Row-height trials use the same capped width as final text; a second arrangement can spend half the default gap on additional lines while preserving any authored gap. Candidates keep every text size and family, complete content, body fill and overflow guards. This changes the drawing of affected under-capacity prose without changing its authored content or adding a document option.

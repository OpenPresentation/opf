LH-01 (output-changing for slides on the section, statement, quote and closing layouts): a layout record can name a background variant in `design.variant`, and the default section draws on the accent, statement and quote on light2 and closing on dark1. A layout's variant outranks the deck's and the theme's variant or background (it hides their background, and keeps the deck's accent order when it names none); the slide's own `design.variant` or `design.background` still wins. A variant's background can now be an accent (`accent1` to `accent6`, `ColorVariantSlot`): the accent is bg1 and bg2 and the text is the scheme's light family (light1, light2) or dark family (dark1, dark2), whichever reads better on it, with tx2 on tx1's slot where the family's tx2 is under 4.5:1. Core resolves it once (`resolveSlideContext` `resolved.roles` and `resolved.layoutVariant`, the validator's contrast checks, `composeSlide`'s background picture); the preview draws the variant's bg1 and the PPTX export writes the slide's `clrMapOvr` (`bg1="accent1"` for an accent) and imports it back as the variant its layout already gives. New on `/composition`: `layoutColorVariant`, `layoutVariantApplies`, the `ColorBaseSlot` type; `colorMapFor` takes the colour scheme as a second argument for accent variants. The preview no longer paints a theme-slot background that sits beside a variant at the same level (`{ "background": "light1", "variant": "dark1" }` drew light text on white): the slide is the variant's bg1, as core and the PPTX export already resolved it.

Four metrics with icons in two rows no longer set the lower row's icon chips on the upper row's labels. The even row spacing of a metrics body (RR-105) measured each row without the chip band its cells reserved above the figures, so on a full body the space between the rows was smaller than the chip band. The rows are now spaced with their chips; no text changes size.

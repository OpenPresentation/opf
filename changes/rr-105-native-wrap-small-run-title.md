Right-aligned text grows to the left and centered text on both sides, so the text stays where the preview draws it; a body that no width keeps keeps its measured lines with `native-body-fallback` reason `tight-wrap`. Separately, a rich line whose runs are all smaller than its fit size (a title of 17 to 20 pt runs under a 40.5 pt title role) is written with exact line spacing whose pitch puts PowerPoint's first baseline (three quarters of the pitch below the top) on the preview's baseline; at single spacing PowerPoint took the baseline from the small runs and drew the line about 22 pt higher.

MX1 (output-changing for a `matrix` of other than four entries): three or six entries drew as gray list cards with each entry at a card's top left, three of them as wide strips one under another, so the slide lost the matrix's look. The cards now take the matrix's primary paint with their entries centered, as the four quadrants do, and among the grids that hold the entries at one size the one whose cards are nearest 1.25 times as wide as tall is kept. The PPTX export writes them as before (native shapes, or OPF's card-grid SmartArt for one to six entries) in the new paint. A matrix whose entries have descriptions still draws as a list.

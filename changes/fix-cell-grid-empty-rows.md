Blocks in cells (output-changing for slides whose cells leave a row or column empty between others): a row or column of a slide's 3 x 3 cell grid in which no block begins or ends, with a block wholly on each side of it, now takes no space, so `top:...` and `bottom:...` blocks split the body into two rows (and `start` and `end` into two columns, four corners into quarters) instead of leaving an empty band across the middle of the slide; charts, pictures, tables and lists grow into the larger cells and text keeps its size or grows. A block that runs through such a row (`start` beside `top:end` and `bottom:end`) still spans the full height. An unnamed row or column at the edge keeps its third, so a lone `bottom:end` block still sits bottom end. When the middle row gives its space away, a bottom-row block with no top-row block above it (a diagonal, a corner) sits at the foot of its cell, so it stays at the bottom of the body; one under a top-row block keeps the top of its cell and follows that block. The editor's region layouts "Side by side", "Stacked", "One on top, two below", "One on the left, two on the right" and "Two by two" now fill the body. In the repository this redraws two slides of `docs/fixtures/numbered-lists.opf.json` and one of the Markdown kitchen-sink test fixture; no golden and no gallery example changes.

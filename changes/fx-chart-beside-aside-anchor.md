The `chart-beside` layout sets its aside in the middle of the aside's column, level with the chart, instead of at the top. The aside is as tall as the chart, so a short takeaway, a short list, a metric or a quote stood in the top third of the column with the rest empty; the record's `aside` region now says `anchor: "middle"`. A quote aside keeps its card and its quote mark: a lone quote stacked at a middle or bottom anchor is measured with its card's insets, so it no longer loses the card for lack of room.

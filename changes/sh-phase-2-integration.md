Shapes phase 2 integration (SH-04 overlay shapes and connectors, SH-05 PPTX shape import, SH-06 editor shapes, SH-07 native Mermaid diagrams), where the four meet. One connector writer: the PPTX export writes SH-04's overlay connectors and SH-07's glued diagram edges as the same native `p:cxnSp`, glued with `a:stCxn` and `a:endCxn` to the native shape ids at core's connection site indexes (`shapeSites`; a diagram node of custom geometry has a rectangle's four sites in the same order). On import, a diagram group the export wrote (`OPF diagram N` with an intact `OPF_DIAGRAM_V1` manifest) is its Mermaid diagram again and none of its members become overlay shapes or connectors, also beside overlays in front of and behind it; an edited group imports as its shapes, as a PowerPoint deck's shapes do (the nodes as overlay shapes with their presets, fills, lines and text, the glued edges as connectors between them) and reports `invalid-diagram-provenance`. The grouped-text-reflow finding skips intact diagram groups. `docs/shapes.md` is one page: shape blocks, overlays and connectors, import, editing, native diagrams, then validation, loading, the preset geometry and the property map (with the overlay layer, connector middle and native diagram rows). The MCP's README lists `search_icons` and `search_shapes`; the editing skill names the editor release that has the shape APIs (0.33).

The `pillars` and `team` layouts set a card's content in the middle of its card instead of low in it. Their records had `anchor: "bottom"`: a row of labeled cards (a heading over its text) stood in the lower part of the cards with an empty band above, a short text between pictures sat alone at its card's foot, and short display-word pillars sat below the middle of the body. The records now say `anchor: "middle"`, as `two-column` and `three-column` do; the cards, their shared line and the hug-and-center behavior of short body text are unchanged.

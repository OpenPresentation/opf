LH-130, LH-148: covers and sections set their display title low at the start edge, as Claude Design does. A heading region of a layout record takes its own `anchor` (`bottom` sets the heading group at the foot of a sized area; `middle` stays the default) and the `title` region takes `rule: true`, a short accent rule (80 by 5 px) half a gap above a title that has no tag, at the start edge of its first line; a tag stays the group's eyebrow and takes the rule's place, and the rule is drawn only where the headings are the slide's message (no body), so it never costs a body its room. Core composes it as `SlideComposition.titleRule`; the preview draws it as a rectangle and the PPTX export as a native rectangle tagged `OPF_TITLE_RULE_V1` (import consumes it), both in the tag's color. The gallery `cover` and `section` use `anchor: "bottom"` and `rule: true` with start alignment; the section keeps its `accent1` variant (a fixed deck background still wins, LH-103). A title that is the slide's whole message (a cover, a section, a closing) and fits one line at its role size may now take a second line as it grows to its 75 px display size, when the two lines read as one balanced block (the shorter at least half the longer, no line but the last ending in a one-letter word or an article), so a long cover or section title reaches display size instead of stopping a step or two below it. LH-148: a heading that LH-129 grows keeps the line breaks WI-4 balanced at its role size, so "A smaller pilot. / A clearer decision." no longer turns into "A smaller pilot. A / clearer decision." at the grown size: growth stops at the largest step that keeps the breaks.

SH-04: overlay shapes and connectors. A slide's `shapes` array is drawn over the composed slide, above the content and the furniture, in array order, and never moves content. An overlay shape has the shape block vocabulary (preset or `path`, `adjust`, fill, stroke, style, the effects, `rotate`, `flip`, text with `anchor`, `padding` and `textDirection`, `alt`, `decorative`, `hidden`, `link`) and one placement: `x`, `y`, `width` and `height` (fractions of the slide; `x` from the right edge in a right-to-left deck) or `at: { block, edge, offset, width, height }` against a composed block (`top`, `bottom`, `start`, `end` or `center`), so it follows the block. A connector (`{ connector: { from, to, route, fromSite, toSite }, stroke }`) joins two blocks or overlay shapes at connection sites (a preset's own, a rectangle's four for any other block), chosen automatically as the facing pair with the shortest route; `route` is `straight`, `elbow` or `curved`. Core composes them as `SlideComposition.overlayShapes`: overlay shapes as shape block items (`kind: "shape"`, SH-02's `ComposedShape`, text fitted by OPF's measured fonts) and connectors as `ComposedConnector`s routed as PowerPoint's `straightConnector1`, `bentConnector2`/`bentConnector3` and `curvedConnector2`/`curvedConnector3` with their `a:xfrm` frame and `a:avLst`, and the same route in slide coordinates. New findings: `opf/shape-anchor-missing` and `opf/connector-end-missing`; an overlay shape with both placements or none, and a site index the shape does not have, are `opf/schema` errors. A schema error in a union of object forms is now reported against the form that knows more of the value's properties.

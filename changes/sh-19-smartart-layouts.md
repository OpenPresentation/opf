SH-19: OPF's own designs as live SmartArt on OPF's own layout definitions. Where PowerPoint's built-in layouts do not draw an arrangement, OPF draws its own design (the stepped pyramid, a matrix's card grid, wrapped and snaking chevrons and flows, and the ring, loops and quadrants that stay native for now); the PPTX export now writes such a design as live, editable SmartArt on a layout definition OPF authors from ECMA-376 and its design, never from PowerPoint's parts (`ppt/diagrams/layoutN.xml`, `r:lo` naming it), with SH-16's fallback picture. The definition is a table of explicit constraints that PowerPoint lays out with no algorithm of its own: a `composite` root with one branch per entry count (so a node added or deleted in PowerPoint keeps OPF's design), every node at `l`/`t`/`w`/`h` as shares of the frame, a hidden full-frame child, axis-aligned arrows turned by `rot` and other arrows as connectors between node centers, the text at OPF's size (`primFontSz` for added nodes) and anchored where OPF's composition starts its first line. Its `uniqueId` is `urn:openpresentation.org/smartart/layout/<design>/<digest>` (PowerPoint keeps the first definition of a `uniqueId` for its whole session, so each distinct table has its own), with the design's title and description ("OPF Stepped Pyramid", "OPF Card Grid", ...). Core adds OPF's interpreter of that subset (`evaluateLayoutDefinition`, `layoutArrangement`, `compositeLayoutDefinition`, `opfLayoutGeometry`, `presetTextRect`, `turnedShapeBox`), which returns a composed arrangement's nodes and arrows and matches what PowerPoint drew through COM within 0.001 pt; the export writes a definition only when the interpreter draws the composed design from it within 0.1 pt. A composed OPF design now carries the recipe of its design (`ComposedArrangement.layout`) and, for the domain, its line-break stability and text density (`smartArt.stable`, `fill`, `ink`). The domain (core `SMART_ART_OPF_DOMAIN`, `opfSmartArtUnavailable`) is what the COM sweep passed: the stepped pyramid 1 to 8 entries, the card grid 1 to 6, chevron and flow rows 6 to 8, the vertical flow's columns 5 to 6, Latin left-to-right entries of at most two stable lines, text covering at most 7.5% of the frame; everything else stays native shapes with `pptx/smartart-unavailable`. Import reads OPF's layouts back to their arrangement by `uniqueId`, from the `mc:Choice`, from the fallback picture alone and after PowerPoint saved the file again. See `docs/smartart.md`.

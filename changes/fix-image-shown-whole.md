The `image` layout's lone picture shown whole (its proportions known and not cropped to cover the slide) or with a caption keeps the body inside the margins and above the footer, in the middle of it, instead of bleeding to the slide's edges: a panorama no longer sits at the top over an empty lower half, a picture no longer runs under the footer text, and a caption starts at the picture's edge right under it instead of at the slide's left edge.

LH-186 (output-changing for dense slides only): composition changes the arrangement before it sets text smaller. A paragraph, list or table set more than one step of the type scale (LH-149's half steps) under its role size (28, 32 and 25 px) tries the arrangements that keep it larger, under the new guarded rule `layout-first`: a long list (more than six plain entries, no `columns` from its payload or region, the slide's only list) is dealt into two or three columns of at least 36 characters when that sets it at least one step larger (ten 12-word entries: 21 px in one column, 26 px in two), and a layout template is composed as LH-83's automatic grid above the floor too (the agenda-split layout's two long lists: 18 px beside the title, 32 px across the slide; `recomposed` reports it). An arrangement is kept only when it adds no finding, sets no block smaller, reads as well and sets the most shrunk block at least one step larger, so dense content that no arrangement holds (a 14-entry list, a 160-word paragraph, a 14-row table) keeps its fitted size. `fill: false`, `recompose: false`, a block pinned to a region, a payload's `columns`, a run's own `fontSize` and SmartArt-style arrangements keep their composition. No example deck or layout sampler slide changes; 13 of core's 432 template goldens and 15 of the renderer's layout-template goldens do.

The `dashboard` layout's KPI row is one row of metrics over the charts (C05). Its `lead` region flowed as a grid, so four metrics were dealt 2 x 2 into the row's height and set at the floor, and the slide gave way to the automatic grid with the fourth metric alone beside squeezed charts; the region now says `flow: "row"`, so four or five metrics stand side by side and the charts keep the full width. One or two metrics in a layout's metric-only row are centered in their cells as three are, at their display size, instead of start-aligned as a figure beside a chart (LH-112).

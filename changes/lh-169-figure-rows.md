LH-169, LH-170 (output-changing): figure-led lists draw as a row of metrics, and a closing note draws on the emphasis band, as Claude Design draws narrative n15 and n18. A list of 2 to 4 entries that each lead with a short figure (`figureText`: a number with its unit or comparison, at most 4 words, such as "62 minutes a day", "1 in 6 reports", "$3.2M" or "٦٢ دقيقة يوميًا"; not a step number, a clock time, a year or a date) and carry a description (`figureList`) draws one column per entry, in reading order (from the right in a right-to-left deck): the figure bold in the accent at display size over an accent rule, one size for the row and always on one line, and its description as the label under it at the size the list draws its descriptions (fitList `labelScale`). A list where only some entries lead with a figure, or whose figures have no descriptions, stays a list. A lone short note that closes the body becomes the full-width foot band whatever its length in characters, when it sets in at most 3 lines (`EMPHASIS_NOTE_LINES`): the text of a layout's `verdict` region, or the last block after two or more figures (metrics or figure lists), under them or beside them; a featured pair keeps its card. Both are guarded rules (`figure-row`, `note-band`): no block, label or note reads smaller than without them, and the band's text keeps the size the note has on the slide without it. Both engines draw them natively (a list in columns and the existing foot band). Over the 992 sweep slides no block is smaller and no finding is added.

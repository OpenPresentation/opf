LH-06 and LH-68 (layout harvest; output-changing): one emphasis surface per slide, and scorecard metrics sized from their tiles.

- **`emphasis` on a block or group** (schema, core, render, PPTX). `emphasis: true` draws the block or group on the slide's emphasis surface: a card in its cell, with no card of its own for anything drawn on it. Its colours come from the deck's own scheme slots through the LH-01 variant machinery (new core `emphasisSurface`): on a light slide the brand dark (dark2, then dark1, then the accents), on a dark slide the accents in the slide's order, then light1, the first whose text reaches 4.5:1 on its fill and whose fill reaches 3:1 against the slide. Every text role on the surface (text, secondary text, primary, secondary, accent) under 4.5:1 is drawn in the surface's text colour. A slide has one surface: the first `emphasis: true` in reading order takes it, any other is drawn without it and reported (composition diagnostic `emphasis-ignored`). Composition reports the surface as `SlideComposition.emphasis` (`path`, `box`, `radius`, `shape` card, band or pill, `source`) and marks every item on it `emphasis: true`; the preview and the PPTX export draw it from there (PPTX: a tagged `OPF card` frame with no line, imported as decoration), and `validate` measures the contrast of those items against the surface.
- **Automatic emphasis** (no authoring). On a slide without an authored emphasis, a short last text block (60 characters or fewer) after blocks that are all longer (any other kind, or text over 60 characters) is drawn as a full-width band at the foot of the body, and one of 30 characters or fewer after a lone long text as a pill there, at the start of the content alignment. The body above composes in what is left, with a layout or without; the band is dropped when the body overflows less without it, and `emphasis: false` keeps a block out. Of the 360 composition goldens, 7 cases take a foot band (timeline, metrics, chart, chart-beside, table, table-beside and code with 2 short blocks), none gained a finding.
- **Metric tiles** (layout schema, core, gallery). A layout region's new `metrics: "tile"` lays each metric in it out from its whole cell: the label first as an eyebrow, the value under it asking for a quarter of the cell's height (between its 76 px role size and twice it, new `layoutMetric` option `tile`), a stacked unit under the value, and the description, delta and trend at the foot of the cell, start-aligned. Tiles fill their cards (no hugging) and siblings still share their sizes (LH-43). The gallery `scorecard` layout's `cards` region uses it; its 12 composition golden cases moved.

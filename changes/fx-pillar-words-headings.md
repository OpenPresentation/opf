A set of one-word pillars without descriptions (three headings such as "Scope", "Measures", "Decision") draws each word at display size over an accent rule, as one-word pillars with a short line under them do (LH-136), instead of three thin pills in an empty body (P3).
When a short layout body grows on past LH-48's three steps (LH-102), its display words never come out smaller than the three steps set them.

LH-160, LH-161 (output-changing for charts): a histogram or Pareto chart of a lone value column draws only as many bins as its plot can label one beside another (core `chartHistogram`, `layer.histogram`; Scott's count when there is room, written as `cx:binCount`), its bin edges are rounded to the decimals the bin width needs and written as the category axis number format so PowerPoint keeps each label on one line, and per-bar data labels that would overlap are dropped in the preview, the native part and the classic fallback; the histogram bin helpers `scottBinCount` and `histogramBins` (new `count` and `decimals` options) are core's, the renderer and the exporter re-export them. A pie's or doughnut's last slice, when it would read as one tone with the first, is recolored along the lightness axis by at most 20 L* to 11 L* from the first and from its neighbor (`chartSlicePlan`, every other slice keeps the palette color), or, where no color within 20 L* clears both, keeps its palette color while every slice of the chart gets a 2 px surface-colored border (native as each data point's line in PPTX); `opf/chart-color-only` accepts a bordered wrap pair and reports none on the gallery color schemes, and now holds only a pie or doughnut to the wrap pair (a treemap or funnel has no circle). Over the gallery schemes, 4 surfaces and 3 to 12 slices, 116 of 560 pies are recolored (largest move 16.4 L*), 152 bordered and 292 unchanged; the PPTX export writes a moved slice as an sRGB literal. Gallery records are unchanged, so no `drawing` entry.

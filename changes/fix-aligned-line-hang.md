Right-aligned and centered wrapped text is flush again: the space that ends a soft-wrapped line hangs at the wrap, as PowerPoint and a browser draw it, instead of pushing the line's glyphs one space (centered: half a space) away from the edge. Core records that whitespace on the line (`TextLineSource.hang` and `advance`, the same on `RichTextLine`) and `placeTextLines` aligns the line by its glyphs (its placed `width` is the line's whole advance, `hang` included), so the preview (SVG, PNG and PDF) and the measured PowerPoint export (each line box, and a heading's paragraph margins) put the glyphs of every line of a heading, body text, list, table cell or caption on the same edge. In a right-to-left line the whitespace hangs on the left: a right-to-left line that filled its box is no longer drawn and exported a space short of the right edge, and a centered or left-aligned right-to-left line is drawn with its glyphs centered or on the left edge (its PowerPoint paragraph stays anchored by its glyphs). The text of each line is unchanged, so the editor's trace and the PowerPoint import read it as before; start-aligned left-to-right lines do not move, and a space before a line break or at the end of the text still counts, as it does in PowerPoint. The ink of such a line is measured without its hanging whitespace. The preview names the glyphs' anchor of a left-to-right line whose whitespace hangs in `data-opf-anchor` and `data-opf-anchor-x`, where the canvas editor lays its input, and in `data-opf-hang` how far from it the text with its whitespace is anchored (as the native export anchors its paragraph).

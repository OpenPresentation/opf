Right-aligned and centered wrapped text is flush again: the space that ends a soft-wrapped line hangs at the wrap, as PowerPoint and a browser draw it, instead of pushing the line's glyphs one space (centered: half a space) away from the edge. Core records that whitespace on the line (`TextLineSource.hang` and `advance`, the same on `RichTextLine`) and `placeTextLines` aligns the line by its glyphs (its placed `width` is the line's whole advance, `hang` included), so the preview (SVG, PNG and PDF) and the measured PowerPoint export (each line box, and a heading's paragraph margins) put the glyphs of every line of a heading, body text, list, table cell or caption on the same edge. The text of each line is unchanged, so the editor's trace and the PowerPoint import read it as before; start-aligned lines and lines drawn right to left do not move, and a space before a line break or at the end of the text still counts, as it does in PowerPoint. The preview names the glyphs' anchor of such a line in `data-opf-anchor` and `data-opf-anchor-x`, where the canvas editor lays its input.

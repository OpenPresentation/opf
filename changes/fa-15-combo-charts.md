FA-15: combo charts. A new chart type `combo` (catalog record `combo`, `mappings.openxml.composition: "mixed"`: a clustered column `barChart` and a `lineChart` with markers) draws an amount beside a rate, such as revenue and margin %. `Chart.line` names the series drawn as lines (default: the last series; at least one series stays columns), `Chart.secondaryAxis` puts line series on a secondary value axis at the right, labelled in its first series' column format, and `ChartAxisTitles.secondary` titles that axis. `resolveChartData` returns the plan as `combo` (`{ role, axis }` per series) and orders the series columns first, then the primary-axis lines, then the secondary-axis lines, so the preview, the PPTX export and the legend agree; `resolveChartOptions` returns the line series' label position as `dataLabels.linePosition` and reports `line`, `secondaryAxis` and `axisTitles.secondary` on other chart types as `chart-option-adapted`. An unknown `line` or `secondaryAxis` name is a `chart-mapping-unknown-column` error, one that cannot apply a `chart-mapping-adapted` warning. `check:spec` rule (f) accepts a mixed composition that names the Aspose.Slides type it starts from and the series type it switches to (`lineSeriesType`). `technical/chart-type-sampler.opf.json` gains a revenue and margin combo slide (new coordinated golden `scripts/fixtures/opf-examples-png.combo-chart.sha256.json`). See `docs/chart-options.md#combo-charts`.

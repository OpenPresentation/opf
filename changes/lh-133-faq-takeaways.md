LH-133, LH-134 (output-changing for FAQ, glossary, takeaway and agenda slides): questions, terms and numbers drawn as Claude Design draws them, read from the content and never from a slide id. A list of questions with answers (`questionText`: a text ending in a question mark of its script, `?`, `？`, `؟` or `⸮`) is drawn as ruled rows, each question bold in a column of 40% of the width with its answer beside it (the guarded rule `pair-rows`); a region whose blocks are each one question with its answer (an FAQ written one block per question) stacks them as ruled rows across the region, the answers at one edge and at the list size (`ListFitOptions.rowLabels`), and six or more short questions keep the region's grid with a 3 px accent rule over each cell (`ListRule.accent`, `LIST_ACCENT_RULE_WIDTH`). The slide's lone list of terms with definitions, most of them one word (`pairEntry`, `TERM_WORDS`), is a glossary whose terms draw in the accent (`ListEntryLayout.accent`). A lone numbered list of two to five takeaways draws display numerals in the accent beside ruled rows (2.5 times the list size beside takeaways with a supporting line, `LIST_NUMERAL_SCALE`), and an agenda whose entries all have descriptions draws as numbered ruled rows ("01" at the list size, the description in a second column) instead of LH-07's badges (`ListFitOptions.numerals`, the guarded rule `numerals`, `ComposedStepBadge.numeral`); one rule decides between numerals, badges and plain rows (docs/dynamic-composition.md, "Numerals, badges or rows"), and LH-07's veto holds: the numbers are drawing, an unnumbered agenda imports unnumbered and an authored numbering comes back from each numeral's tag. The preview draws the accent terms, accent rules and numerals, and the PPTX export writes them as run colors, native lines and tagged text boxes at core's geometry; import reads none of them as content and now keeps two stacked lists the export named apart instead of merging them into one.

RR-71 (OPF 0.18, breaking, no aliases): logos live on the organization, and a header or footer zone lays its parts out in one row.
    - `Organization.logo` is one path or Asset for every shape, or `{ full, stacked, icon, wordmark }`, each a path or Asset or `{ onLight, onDark }` (new `$defs` `OrganizationLogo` and `LogoSource`). A missing shape falls back to `full`, and `full` to the first defined of `wordmark`, `stacked` and `icon`; a missing `onLight` or `onDark` uses the other.
    - Removed: the `LogoSet` `$def`, `design.logo` as an asset or LogoSet, and `HeaderFooterItem.logo`. `design.logo` (deck and slide) is now a logo reference (`"var:organization.beta.logo"`, `"var:organization.logo.wordmark"`) that picks which logo covers, sections and picture bullets draw, or `false` for none. Migration: a LogoSet's `default`/`dark`/`light` become `full` with `onLight` (the dark artwork) and `onDark` (the light artwork), and likewise for `stacked*`, `icon*` and `wordmark*`; the deck's `design.logo` moves onto the primary organization's `logo`; a zone's `logo: true` becomes `image: "var:organization.logo.icon"`.
    - Placement goes through the variable system: `var:organization.logo` (the full logo), `var:organization.logo.<shape>`, `var:organization.<id>.logo` and `var:organization.<id>.logo.<shape>`, as a whole field in any image field. They are slide-scoped, like `{{slide.number}}`: `resolveVariables` keeps them where a slide reaches them, `resolveSlideVariables(slide, { slideNumber, slideCount, presentation, darkBackground })` resolves them to the organization's asset for that slide's background (an organization without a logo omits the field), `resolveSlideContext` passes its `darkBackground`, and `layoutFurniture` resolves them in zone `image`. Elsewhere (a deck watermark) they resolve deck-wide as on a light background. An inline `{{organization.logo}}`, an unknown shape or an unknown organization id is `variable-unknown-builtin`; an organization without a logo is `variable-builtin-missing`. `listBuiltinVariables` lists every logo shape with scope `slide`, and `organization.logo` is no longer a deck-wide built-in.
    - `resolveLogo(presentation, slide, { shape, onDark, slideIndex, reference })` implements the one rule (`shape` replaces `slot`); `ResolvedLogo` and `ComposedLogo` carry `shape`, `variant` (`onLight`, `onDark` or `default`) and the canonical `reference`; `LOGO_SHAPES` is exported from the root.
    - Zones: the parts of a header or footer zone sit side by side in the order image, text, socials, date (mirrored right to left), aligned to the zone's edge with a `FURNITURE_GAP` of 12 reference pixels, each vertically centered on the row; `\n` still breaks lines inside a part. A lone text part keeps the full zone width. Beside other parts an image is at most `FURNITURE_IMAGE_SHARE` (40%) of the zone width. When the row is wider than the zone its text parts wrap at an equal share (narrow parts keep their width); a word that would break inside itself at the readability floor is a `text-overflow` diagnostic at the part's text, never a silent break. `FurniturePart.field` drops `logo`, and an image part from a logo reference carries `reference`. **Geometry-moving change** wherever a zone has more than one part; in the bundled examples only `technical/header-footer-logo-set.opf.json` (its footer zone draws the logo icon beside the organization name).

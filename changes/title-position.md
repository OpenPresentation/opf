Breaking (0.35 layout simplification, step 6): new design key `titlePosition`, `top` (the default: the heading group above the body) or `start`: the tag, title and subtitle beside the body, in a column of two fifths of the content width on the reading side (the right in a right-to-left deck; `design.mirror` swaps it), centered down the column at the title's role size. It resolves like every shared design key (the slide, the deck, the theme, the layout record, then `top`) and works on any layout and on an automatic slide: on a template the rows that hold only headings give way and the title column runs beside the body regions, which keep their own grid (core `titleBesideTemplate`, `TITLE_BESIDE_SHARE` 2 : 3; a `subtitle` region joins the title's group; blocks no region takes still sit below, across the slide; a record whose title already shares a row with a body region keeps its placement); an automatic slide composes its body beside the column. A cover, a slide with promoted regions, a portrait canvas and a slide that LH-83 recomposes as the automatic grid keep the title on top. The gallery's `cycle`, `matrix` and `vertical-process` become column records with `design.titlePosition: "start"` and draw exactly as before (the render template goldens: every remaining slide PNG- and SVG-identical; the example corpus: identical except the layout sampler's slide 4); a deck or slide that sets `titlePosition: "top"` now puts their title on top. `agenda-split` is removed (38 layouts; the `drawing` declaration is the removed id): it is `agenda` with `titlePosition: "start"`. A slide with `layout: "agenda-split"` resolves nowhere and composes automatically; `opf/unresolved-reference` says what replaced it and offers the safe fix `rename-layout`, which writes `agenda` and adds the slide's `design.titlePosition: "start"` (core `renamedLayout` and the new `renamedLayoutTitlePosition`). The layout sampler's `agenda-split` slide is now an automatic slide with its title beside its list. The editor adds the Title position control (design option `titlePosition`, deck and slide). PPTX export writes a record's `start` as a slide layout whose title placeholder is the start column, a slide's own `start` as its title's explicit position, stores `titlePosition` in the document and slide records, writes one slide layout fewer (no Agenda beside), and imports a 0.34 file's `agenda-split` slide (stored layout or layout tag) on `agenda` with `titlePosition: "start"`. `docs/migrations/0.35.md` lists each change.

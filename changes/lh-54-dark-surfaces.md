LH-54, LH-55 and LH-79 (output-changing for dark decks with cards, charts or code, and for light decks with code): dark surfaces read as surfaces.
    - **Cards (LH-54).** `roles.neutrals` gains `card` and `cardBorder`. On a dark slide whose `surface` does not stand off the background (the `dark2` variant, whose bg2 is the background itself) and where no `surface` is authored, a card is the background mixed towards light1 from 7% until 1.25:1 (`#172C52` on the default `#011842`), with a 1 px stroke 12% further towards light1, instead of the background with a hairline. Everywhere else a card is `surface` with `border`, as before. The preview and the PPTX export draw the card from these neutrals, a metric in a card is coloured against the card, and `opf/text-contrast` checks text in a card against it.
    - **Chart series (LH-55).** On a dark chart panel every series from the scheme reaches 4.5:1 (only series 1 did), and series 2 keeps a lightness step of at least 20 (CIE L*) from series 1: on the default navy the second series is `#93BEE7`, not `#3F6B88` (3.0:1). `chartPaletteForFill`'s `legibleLead` option is replaced by `legible` (one flag per series, from the new `chartSchemeSeries`), `chartLeadFromScheme` by `chartSchemeSeries`, and `CHART_LEAD_MIN_CONTRAST` by `CHART_DARK_SERIES_MIN_CONTRAST`; `CHART_LEAD_LIGHTNESS_STEP` is new. Authored `chart.colors` entries keep the 3:1 floor.
    - **Code panels (LH-79).** On a light deck whose dark1 is near-black the code panel is dark2 (a navy theme draws a navy panel, not a black one); on a dark deck it is the background at 70% of its lightness (`schemeClr bg1` with `lumMod 70000` in PPTX), a step below the slide, instead of bg2, which on `dark2` was the background itself. Syntax colours keep 4.5:1 against the new panel.

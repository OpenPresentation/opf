Five font schemes that pair a display face with a different text face, all from the open families opf-render bundles and PowerPoint read back natively: `anton-figtree` (a heavy condensed display face over a geometric sans, for short headlines and big numbers), `playfair-display-lora` (a high-contrast display serif over a text serif, for narrative decks), `bitter-source-sans-3` (a slab serif over a humanist sans, for headings that carry across a room), `archivo-narrow-lora` (a narrow grotesque that keeps long action titles on one line, over a text serif) and `roboto-mono-figtree` (monospace headings and code over a geometric sans, for technical decks). Every earlier record sets one family for both slots.

LH-44 (output-changing for slides with tables): tables keep whole words and readable rows. A table row is never shorter than its text at the readability floor plus its insets: a table that cannot fit steps its type down to the floor and then runs past its box and reports `text-overflow`, instead of squeezing its rows over their text. A word wider than its column at the floor is overflow too (`layoutTable` still sets `splitsWords`). `TableLayout` reports `naturalWidth` (every cell on one line), `minimumWidth` (each column's longest word) and `naturalHeight` (the rows at the settled size). In automatic composition a table beside other blocks in a row takes its natural width, up to 65% of the row, when its neighbors keep their fit, and in a `row` composition it takes at least the width its words need; an automatic or `grid` arrangement (with no column count) that would leave a table narrower than its words places it at its natural width beside a column of the other blocks, or across the whole width between rows of them, whichever scores better (decision reason `table-width`). Stacked tables, in templates and in columns, are measured: they get at least their rows at the floor before other blocks shrink, and keep the spare height as before. The preview and the PPTX export read the same geometry.

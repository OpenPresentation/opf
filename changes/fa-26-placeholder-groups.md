FA-26: layout records can nest placeholder groups. A `placeholders` entry may be `{ "type": "group", "composition", "placeholders" }`: the group arranges its regions inside one cell of the record's composition with the existing `Composition` object (only `minFontSize` and `overflow` inherit), holds body regions and groups only, and nests at most three levels (`MAX_PLACEHOLDER_GROUP_DEPTH`; the schema rejects a fourth level and `validate` reports it as the new rule `opf/layout-placeholder-group`). Slide content stays flat and fills the record's leaf regions in reading order; a slide's own `composition` still overrides the record per key at the root, and a slide with its own content groups or promoted regions keeps its structure. `composeSlide` reports the record's slots (`geometry.slots`: path, kind, depth, cell and the content that fills it) and pagination composes each page through the groups. New exports from `@openpresentation/opf/composition`: `layoutLeaves`, `layoutSlots`, `layoutStructure`, `isPlaceholderGroup`, `hasPlaceholderGroups`, `chartPrimaryLayout` and the `ComposedSlot` type; `layoutContent` counts leaf regions. `design.chartPrimary` stays accepted and is now implemented as the placeholder group it stands for (`chartPrimaryLayout`), with unchanged geometry. The catalog snapshot mirrors pptx.gallery's rewritten records: the six `chart-3x-*` primary layouts state their primary chart and supporting group explicitly instead of setting `design.chartPrimary`; `chart-2x`, `chart-3x`, `image-2x` and `image-3x` pair each chart or image with its note in one column; `dashboard`, `kpi-scorecard`, `market-size`, `org-chart`, `business-model-canvas`, `logic-tree` and `capability-map` hold the structure their descriptions promise. Embedded records in documents are unchanged, so no example deck or golden changes. `docs/fixtures/placeholder-groups.opf.json` and `pnpm test:placeholder-groups` check that core, the renderer and the PPTX exporter place every leaf within 0.5 pt.

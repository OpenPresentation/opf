PR1 (output-changing for the `process` layout): one list of three to five sentence-length steps (too long for Basic Process) drew as a small column of badged entries in the corner of an empty body, while the same steps written as separate blocks drew the layout's row of badged steps. Such a list of 2 to 5 entries of one level now draws as that row: one column per entry across the `steps` region, a numbered badge over each at its start (the right in a right-to-left deck) joined by the connector (its badges and connector grow with the text), the entries under their badges, the row in the middle of the region, the text at the list's size or larger (up to 56 px, each grown entry on at most three lines of whole words). A list whose columns would be narrower than six ems keeps its badges down the side. Both engines draw it as a list in columns with LH-07's row of badges.

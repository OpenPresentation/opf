LH-48, LH-65, LH-89 (output-changing for slides whose body fills under half of its height, layouts with an empty column between filled ones, and nested groups without a gap of their own): a body that fills too little of the slide steps up instead of sitting small at the top of an empty body. The only block of a slide (a paragraph, list, quote, code panel, metric or table, with nothing else in its body) grows on the type scale, each role up to its display size (`LONE_DISPLAY_SIZES`), to about 60% of the body, and sits in its middle with its card hugging it; a grown table's rows grow with its type (`items[].tableGrowth`, which the preview and the PPTX export pass to `layoutTable`). A body of two or more paragraphs and lists in regions that fills under half is composed again up to three type-scale steps larger at one shared size, keeping the largest step that adds no finding and fills at most 60%, and centers under the headings with its cards hugging. A template's only block takes the whole body when every other region is empty. A layout column that holds only empty regions gives way when two or more columns with content remain, so two blocks in `three-column` draw 1:1 (LH-65). A nested group without a `gap` of its own sets its blocks the slide's gap apart (0.25 in at 16:9) instead of 1/30 of its own shorter edge (LH-89). Stacked records (statement, quote, closing), blocks below the grid, captioned blocks, bodies with media and overflowing compositions are unchanged; `composeSlide(slide, { fill: false })` keeps the role sizes and places.

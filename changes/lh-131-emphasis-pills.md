LH-131, LH-137 (output-changing for slides with a before and after or problem and solution pair, a marked pricing tier, a metric delta or a short list on a dark slide): composition finds the featured card of a pair or a set from content alone (core `featuredCells`: "after", "the solution" or a tier marked "recommended" or "most popular", in fifteen languages, never from ids) and draws it on the LH-06 emphasis surface (`emphasis.source: 'featured'`) with the other cells on plain cards; a before and after pair of metrics side by side gets an accent arrow between its cards (`emphasis.arrow`). A metric's delta draws as a pill with its trend arrow beside the value, or above it, where every field keeps its size (`layoutMetric` `deltaPill`, `MetricLayout.deltaPill`, core `metricPillFill`). A dark slide whose body is one short bulleted list draws its entries as check-marked tiles (`steps` form `tiles`, `ComposedStepBadge.check`, core `checkMarkPoints`). All three are guarded rules (`featured-card`, `delta-pill`, `check-tiles`), never cost a block its size, and an authored `emphasis: true` always wins. The preview and the PPTX export draw the arrow, pill, badges and check marks as native shapes (preset arrows, rounded rectangles, ellipses and a custom geometry), tagged and consumed on import.

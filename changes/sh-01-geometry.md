SH-01: preset shape geometry. `@openpresentation/opf/shape-geometry` evaluates the 187 DrawingML preset shapes (ECMA-376 `ST_ShapeType`) at a size and a set of adjust values: `PRESET_SHAPES`, `presetAdjustments` (defaults and handle ranges as OPF fractions and degrees), `presetGeometry` (SVG paths with their fill modes and stroke flags, the text rectangle, connection sites and adjust handles), `customGeometry` (SVG path data in a 0 to 1 box or a viewBox), `adjustToDrawingML`/`adjustFromDrawingML` and `evaluateGuideFormula`. The data is `spec/reference/preset-geometry.json`, generated by `scripts/build-preset-geometry.mjs` from the standard's `presetShapeDefinitions.xml` (Apache POI's copy, Apache-2.0); every preset matches PowerPoint's drawing within 1 pixel. See `docs/shapes.md`.

FA-36 (breaking): typos are errors, and errors say what to write. `design`, `design.dimensions`, the theme, solid, gradient and pattern backgrounds, `header` and `footer`, inline `colorScheme` (which declares `name`, written as the PPTX theme's color scheme name) and `fontScheme`, `organization`, `speaker`, inline `audience`, `purpose`, `tone` and `language`, and the object form of a text run no longer accept unknown keys: `"titleAlignmnt"` or a run's `"href"` (it is `link`) used to validate and do nothing. A background gradient needs its `stops`. Custom data belongs in `extensions`. To update a deck, rename or remove the unknown key (the finding names the nearest known one) and add `stops` to a gradient background without them ([0.34 migration](../docs/migrations/0.34.md)). Validation messages name the fix: a shape `fill` written like a background says to write `"fill": "accent1"`, with the patch, instead of eight findings; a background no form accepts lists the forms; `type: "list"` beside `bullets` says to use `items`; a dependent key (`labelPosition` without `label`) says both ways out. Schema descriptions are corrected and `TextRun.fontSize` states that it is in points.

Typos are errors, and errors say what to write. `design`, `design.dimensions`, the theme, solid, gradient and pattern backgrounds, `header` and `footer`, inline `colorScheme` and `fontScheme`, `organization`, `speaker`, inline `audience`, `purpose`, `tone` and `language`, and the object form of a text run no longer accept unknown keys: `"titleAlignmnt"`, `"opactiy"` or a run's `"href"` (it is `link`) used to validate and do nothing. A background gradient needs its `stops`. Custom data belongs in `extensions`; to update a deck, rename or remove the unknown key (the finding names the nearest known one) and add `stops` to a gradient background that has none ([0.34 migration](../docs/migrations/0.34.md)). Validation messages name the fix: a shape `fill` written like a background (`{ "type": "solid", "color": "accent1" }`) is one finding that says to write `"fill": "accent1"`, with the patch, instead of eight; a background no form accepts lists the forms and types; `type: "list"` beside `bullets` says to use `items` or remove `type`; a failed `if`/`then` reports its error once; a dependent key (`labelPosition` without `label`) says both ways out; a short enum lists its values; an option on the wrong kind of block says where it belongs. Schema descriptions are corrected (the chart type is `world`, not `map`; an absent `legend` or `dataLabels`; the three diagram syntaxes; which sides mirror right to left) and carry no internal tracker ids; `TextRun.fontSize` states that it is in points and explains its conversion to reference pixels.

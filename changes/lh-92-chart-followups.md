LH-92, LH-93 and LH-95 (output-changing for nested-ring doughnuts, sibling charts that share a value axis and small one-series column and bar charts): the chart layer names rings, gives siblings one plot height and emphasizes by a stronger shade.
    - **Ring names (LH-92).** A doughnut of two or more rings (a pie or doughnut of several series, LH-60) names each ring's series inside the ring, on its largest slice: the name alone, or before that slice's own label ("FY24, 40%"), in the color that contrasts with the slice. Composition decides it (`ChartLayer.rings`, core `chartRingLabels`); in PPTX it is that slice's `c:dLbl` with `c:showSerName val="1"`, and import does not read it as a data label option. A pie drawn as rings no longer writes `c:dLblPos` (a doughnut has no label position) and its labels contrast with their slices, as the preview draws them.
    - **One plot height (LH-93).** Column, line and area charts that share a value axis (LH-33) now also share one plot height: core lays each plot out as the preview does (`chartPlotInset`) and `shareSiblingPlots` gives the set the shortest plot's height, one top and baseline for charts side by side. The plot is `ChartLayer.plot`, an inset from the chart box; the preview draws its plot there and the PPTX export writes it as the plot area's manual layout (`layoutTarget inner`). `shareSiblingChartAxes` returns the shared sets.
    - **Emphasis by shade (LH-95).** A small one-series column or bar chart draws its last point in a stronger shade of the series color (darker on a light backdrop, lighter on a dark one, 15 L* from it, every bar at 3:1) and the other points in the series color, instead of tinting the others; it now applies in all 84 gallery theme x color scheme x variant combinations (the tint applied in 36). In PPTX the series keeps its color and the emphasized point is one `c:dPt`. `CHART_EMPHASIS_TINT_MIX` and `CHART_EMPHASIS_TINT_MIN_CONTRAST` are removed; `CHART_EMPHASIS_MIN_CONTRAST` replaces the latter.

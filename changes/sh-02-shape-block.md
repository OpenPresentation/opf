SH-02 (breaking: picture `border` is now `stroke`, image `shape` names and `cornerRadius` changed): shape blocks. A block with `shape` (any of DrawingML's 187 presets: `chevron`, `rightArrow`, `wedgeRectCallout`, `flowChartDecision` and the rest) and no `image`, or with a custom `path` (SVG path data), draws the shape with optional `text` inside, and composes like any block: the largest frame of the preset's proportions (or `aspectRatio`) in its cell, its text fitted in the preset's text rectangle by OPF's measured fonts and never below `minFontSize` (`opf/text-overflow` at `<block>/text` otherwise). It takes `adjust` (the preset's handles in fractions and degrees), `fill` (`none`, a color, `{gradient}` with `type` linear, radial, rectangle or shape, `{pattern}`), `opacity`, `stroke` (color or gradient, width, preset or custom `dash`, `cap`, `join`, `miterLimit`, `compound`, `align`, arrowheads `start` and `end`, `sketch`), `style` (none, subtle, moderate by default, intense: PowerPoint's theme shape styles, written as `p:style`), `rotate`, `flip`, `anchor`, `padding`, `textDirection`, `alt`, `decorative`, `hidden` and `link`; the effect keys (`shadow`, `innerShadow`, `glow`, `softEdge`, `reflection`, `blur`) are in the format for SH-03 to draw. The preview draws core's evaluated geometry (SH-01) with every fill mode and stroke property, and draws gradients as PowerPoint 365 does (rectangle gradients as concentric rectangles and shape gradients along the outline, centered on the bounds of the filled paths, with PowerPoint's measured exceptions and its color mixing); a sketched line (`stroke.sketch`) is an outline core computes from a seed, which the preview draws and the PPTX stores in `ask:lineSketchStyleProps`, where PowerPoint draws it as stored. In a browser or edge bundle the preset geometry loads on demand (`ensureShapes`, `shapesPending`), so a deck without shapes or masked pictures beyond the four built-in masks does not carry it; the PPTX export writes a native `p:sp` with `a:prstGeom` (or `a:custGeom`), `a:ln`, `p:style` and the shape's own text body, never autofit. Every ColorRef also takes `{ "color": ..., "lighten": 0.4 }` or `{ "color": ..., "darken": 0.25 }` (PowerPoint's "Lighter 40%"), exported as `a:lumMod`/`a:lumOff` on the base color. An image block's line is `stroke` (renamed from `border`, no alias) with the same vocabulary, its `shape` takes every preset (`rectangle`, `rounded` and `circle` became `rect`, `roundRect` and `ellipse`) and `adjust` replaces `cornerRadius`. New findings: `opf/shape-adjust-unknown`, `opf/shape-adjust-range`, `opf/shape-link-hidden`, `opf/shape-option-unsupported-payload`. Docs: `docs/shapes.md` (with the property map and the Aspose names); example `examples/technical/shapes.opf.json`.

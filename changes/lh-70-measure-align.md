Layout harvest LH-70, LH-72, LH-67 and LH-78 (output-changing: some slides of the text, statement, closing, section, quote, metrics, comparison and hero layouts and of automatic composition draw differently; no gallery record changes). Readable size wins over the ideal measure: `composeSlide` keeps these rules on a slide only when no block's fitted body size gets smaller than without them, unless they remove an overflow (it tries each rule on its own when the set does not pass).

- **Paragraph columns (LH-70).** Three to six sibling paragraphs of similar length (100 to 240 characters each, the longest less than twice the shortest) are set as a grid of 2 to 4 columns, each 45 to 65 average characters wide, at the largest size of the type scale at which the narrowest column keeps 45 characters and the rows fit, when that is as large as the region's own arrangement: six long paragraphs stacked at 21 px become two columns at 21 px. Composition explains it as `paragraph-columns`.
- **Centered layouts center only short text (LH-72).** When the content alignment comes from the layout record or the theme, a paragraph or list over two lines or 120 characters is start-aligned (at the right in a right-to-left deck) in a column at most 65 characters wide, centered in its cell; its siblings follow it, so a grid reads with one alignment. An alignment the slide or deck sets is kept. `opf/centered-body` fires on 0 of the 844 corpus slides (44 before) and on 1 of 862 example slides (1 before: a slide that sets `contentAlignment: center` itself).
- **Lead plus stack (LH-67).** Of three to five sibling texts or quotes, a paragraph of at least 100 characters, at least 2.5 times the median length of the others and twice the next longest, leads: a cell 55% of the width at full height, a text at the lead size, beside a stack of the others at one shared size. A chart or picture with two compact siblings (metrics, or texts and quotes of 120 characters or fewer) leads the same way at 58%. Automatic composition and `flow: auto` or fixed regions only; a `flow: grid` region and an authored `composition.mode` keep their grid. Composition explains it as `lead-stack`.
- **Quotes beside other blocks (LH-78).** A quote beside a block of another kind, or among three or more quotes, starts at the lead size (32 px) and steps down to fit in four lines, never below the body size (28 px), on a card that hugs it, when that makes no quote smaller; otherwise it keeps its size, on a card when the card costs none. A card the slide does not otherwise draw is left out when its inset would make the quote overflow. A lone quote, and one or two quotes alone on a slide, keep the display size. Sibling quotes in one row share one body size; a row of wider cells keeps its own. `layoutQuote` takes `bodySize` and `bodyLines` (`QuoteBodyOptions`).

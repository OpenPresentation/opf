LH-58, LH-82, LH-73, LH-46, LH-47 (output-changing, layout harvest): composition groups a slide's blocks before it places a cell (`groupBinding`, `repeatedPairs`, `majorityKind` on `/composition`; rules over kinds, counts and text lengths, never a layout id). A sequence that repeats a pair (metric, chart, table, picture, video or code with a paragraph, or a long and a short paragraph) is one cell per pair, the pair side by side or stacked as its cell allows. A flowing region that is mostly one kind keeps only that kind: the other blocks it took as the overflow region join the region that accepts them (the note of `metrics` and `chart`) in one row under the grid, at most 30% of the content box, or the row below the grid; with no layout, interleaved kinds with unequal counts are a majority grid above a row of the others. A block that went below the grid joins the region that accepts it: a `flow: none` region takes up to 6 (no `layout-unplaced`), the side-by-side columns of `comparison` and `two-column` become one region of N cells in reading order, a lone fixed region sets pictures as a row or a lead plus mosaic (cover-cropped unless the block, slide, deck or theme sets a fit) and text as a lead over cards; a flowing region takes up to 12 beyond its `max` (still `layout-unplaced`). Grids have balanced rows (9 as 3 x 3, 10 as 4 + 3 + 3, `gridFlowShape`) and the cells of a short row stretch to the full width, except in a grid of pictures, whose cells keep one aspect. The layout records are unchanged; slides with more blocks than their regions' places, and grids of 5 or 7 to 12, draw differently.

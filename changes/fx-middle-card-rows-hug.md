A row of cards with heading lines (labeled list entries) under a middle anchor (the `pillars`, `team`, `two-column` and `three-column` layouts) hugs its tallest card and centers in the body, instead of drawing full-height cards whose lower part is empty (P1). The cards keep one height and their content starts on one line. A bottom-anchored row, and a row with a picture, a quote or a metric, keep their filled cards.

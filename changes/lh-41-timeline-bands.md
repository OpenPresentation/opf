LH-41 (output-changing for every timeline): timelines draw as compact horizontal bands, as Claude Design does. A timeline whose `when` and `what` labels fit their event spacing is one band (`arrangement: "band"`): each `when` an eyebrow above its marker, the `what` and description below; labels alternate above and below, now up to two spacings wide, only when one is wider than its spacing. `TimelineLayout.bounds` gives what the timeline covers, and composition measures a timeline's height with it, label bands included, instead of a fixed 170 px, so a stack never squeezes it under its labels. Several timelines in one template region (the timeline layout, the process layout) are a stack of bands one gap apart, in one column while it fits at the readability floor and otherwise two columns, every band at one size and never a vertical rail; with four or more they are compact (`compact`: smaller role sizes, `when` and `what` on one row). The timeline layout's `timeline` region now flows as a column, so a second or third timeline joins the first instead of being drawn below the notes, and the notes come after the bands. A lone timeline in its region grows its type by up to 4 reference px when it still fits. New `layoutTimeline` options: `horizontal`, `compact`, `inlineHeader` and `sizeOffset`.

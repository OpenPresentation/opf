opf#1492 (output-changing for horizontal bar charts with data labels, and stacked horizontal bar charts with chart options, below a header row): the plot area is written as the plot the preview draws (`layoutTarget inner`), as every other bar and column chart with a manual plot area already was, instead of an outer box. Keynote reads a manual box as the inner plot whatever its target says, so it drew the category names of these charts past the chart's left edge, cut off ("Cranes" read "ranes"); PowerPoint draws names whole, labels inside the frame and bars of the same lengths in both forms. Core's `chartPlotInset` now lays a small horizontal bar chart's data labels out as the preview does (the room of the widest label beyond the longest bar) and covers stacked and 100% stacked horizontal bars with chart options; the preview and the axes are unchanged. Combo charts and the other chart types are unchanged. A 100% stacked horizontal bar chart on a slide with no other chart and no shape attached to a chart point writes its plot area with the right edge pulled in by what the "100%" axis label lacks (about 2 px at 1280 wide), because PowerPoint moved the whole plot 2.3 px left otherwise; the preview keeps no room for that label, so this one edge is 2 px inside the preview's, and slides with several charts or an attached shape keep the preview's plot.
opf#1493 (output-changing for every wrapped line of a left-to-right text block, heading or caption): a left-to-right line is written without the whitespace that hangs at its soft wrap, in the unshifted box, instead of keeping the space in its run and shifting its box by the space's width. PowerPoint counts a trailing space when it aligns a paragraph and Keynote does not, so right-aligned and centered lines overhung by a space in Keynote; without the space both draw the glyphs where the preview does (PowerPoint's ink edges are the same as before). The line's tag records the whitespace as its soft boundary's separator, so import joins the lines as they were authored. Right-to-left lines, lines with any right-to-left run, the lines of a right-to-left deck, lists, timelines, quotes, image callouts, furniture and captions on media placeholders are exported exactly as before.

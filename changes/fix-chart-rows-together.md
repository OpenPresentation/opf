Output-changing for a horizontal bar chart drawn on the rows of a short table beside it (LH-115): the chart's parts now stand together around the rows. The chart box is the largest 4:3 box of its cell, much taller than a table of a few rows, so its header row stood at the top of the box, its value axis title at the foot and the bars in a thin band between them, with the table floating beside them ("One dataset for chart and table" on openpresentation.org). Core now starts the chart's header row on the table's top line (or right above the first row when the table has no header row), and when the two blocks were centered in their row it centers the table and the chart's parts as one group. The preview draws the value axis title right under the tick labels, where PowerPoint sets an axis title. The chart box, its text size and every other chart keep their layout; a chart with a card, a caption band, an explicit legend, a shared plot, stacked names or reference marks is unchanged.

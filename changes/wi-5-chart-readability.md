WI-5 (P19, output-changing for every column, bar, line and area chart): the value axis follows its length. Intervals are at least 48 reference pixels apart and there are at most eight, so a chart beside text or in one of three columns draws 0 to 100% in steps of 20 and no longer 0 to 90% in tens (`chartIntervalsFor`, `CHART_MIN_TICK_SPACING_PX`, `CHART_MAX_INTERVALS` on `/composition`; the PPTX export writes the same `c:majorUnit`). A chart box with room for it (at least 216 x 288 reference pixels) floors its text at 18 px instead of 16 (`chartTextSize`, `CHART_TEXT_FLOOR_PX`), so three-column and dashboard charts read larger in the preview and in PowerPoint. The preview draws the chart panel without an outer frame (the axes and gridlines draw the plot, and the PPTX chart area never had a line). Zero baselines, explicit axis settings and the other chart types' own scales are unchanged. The tick-label column of the waterfall, histogram and pareto previews is sized for the sparser axis, so "100" no longer wraps after "90".

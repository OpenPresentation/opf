Source editor shape hint (phase 4): Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+Space shows a signature-like tooltip of the object around the caret, and typing `{` or `,` between the members of a slide that has a `layout` shows it by itself. For a slide with a layout the rows are the layout's regions (`title`, then the key that fills each region, then `notes`), each marked `set`, `next` (the first required region the slide has not) or `optional`; a slide without a layout lists its common fields, a block the field of its `type`, a chart its `type` and `data`, `organization` its `id` and `name`, and any other object with a schema shape its required fields first. It works in JSON, YAML and Markdown (a slide with `<!-- slide: layout=pillars -->`, the front matter and `opf-slide` fences; the rows say how Markdown writes each field), follows the caret from member to member, closes on Escape or when the caret leaves the object, does not take the arrow keys, is a real CodeMirror tooltip with `role="tooltip"` and a label, is said in the live region as one sentence ("Slide with layout pillars: title set, items next, notes optional"), uses no animation, and works in a read-only editor, which now takes the keyboard by `tabindex` so its read-only keys (Find, fold, hover, the hint) work. The `hints: false` option of `mountJsonCodeEditor` turns off only the automatic show.

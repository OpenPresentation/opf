LH-144, LH-149, LH-150, LH-151 (layout harvest; output-changing for slides whose body text shrank to fit, paginated slides, ratios such as "4.6 / 5", and metric values on dark cards in validate). Fitted body text takes the half steps of LH-17's type scale too (16, 17, 18, 20, 21, 23, 24, 26, 28, 30 and 32 px at the default floor), so siblings sized jointly lose at most half a step of the size they fit (six FAQ pairs that fit 24 px drew at 21, now 23); it is a guarded rule (`half-steps`), kept only where no block reads smaller, and LH-83 counts the half step above the floor as the floor. New `composition.maxFontSize` (reference pixels, 8 to 200, inherited like `minFontSize`) caps paragraphs, lists (a list in columns keeps the columns its role size chooses), quotes, metrics and timelines, their growth included, never under `minFontSize`: no block grows past it and a block composed larger is laid out again in its own place at the cap; `paginate` (and the export's `continueOverfull`) writes it on every page after the first, set to the largest size the first page draws for the kinds of block the page holds, so a last page with a lone timeline or a short list no longer reads larger than the pages before it. Line breaking keeps a ratio whole (a figure, a slash and a figure: "4.6 / 5", "3/4"), in measurement, preview, table cells and the PPTX export's no-break spaces, and a metric value written so is one figure. `opf/text-contrast` judges a metric value in the color the engines draw (`metricValueColor` on its card or slide), so a value lifted to the light text color on a dark card no longer reports 2.73:1.

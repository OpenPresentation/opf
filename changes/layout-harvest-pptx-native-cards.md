Layout harvest: a text, list or bullets block drawn on a card exports as a native, editable text box at its place inside the card (it does not bind to the slide layout's Body placeholder, so Change Layout cannot move it off its card). Before, any carded block fell back to measured shapes, so the cards of pillars, team, two-column and three-column would have cost editable text.

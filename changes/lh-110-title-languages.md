A chart's reference line (LH-110) is also drawn for a number the title writes in English words ("stayed above ninety-two percent", "under thirty"; labeled in digits) and for titles in German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese and Dutch ("über 92 %", "au-dessus de 92 %", "por encima del 92 %"), with their decimal comma, thousands separators and percent words. The word list is exported as `TITLE_REFERENCE_LANGUAGES`, with `titleReferenceNumbers`, `titleReferenceLanguage` and `englishWordNumber`. Period bands stay English-only.

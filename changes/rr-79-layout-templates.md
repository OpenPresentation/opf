RR-79 (breaking, OPF 0.19, held for the 0.19 train): layouts are grid templates (`opf-layout/v2`; design in `docs/programs/release-readiness/0.19-layouts.md`, reference in `docs/layout-templates.md`). A layout record names a grid with `areas` (the notation of CSS `grid-template-areas`), sizes it with relative `columns` and `rows` (or `auto` rows), and declares each body area as a region in `regions` (`accepts`, `role`, `flow`, `max`, `bleed`, `listColumns`, `anchor`, `empty`), with an optional `overflowRegion`; `composition` keeps only `gap`, `padding`, `minFontSize` and `overflow`.
    - **Binding and flow:** `bindRegions` (on `/composition`) binds a slide's flat content to regions by pins, role rank and reading order, and never drops content (`opf/layout-unplaced`). Regions flow as `none`, `grid` (2, 3, 2 x 2, 3 + 2, 3 x 2, then four columns), `column` or `auto` (a column when a block is longer than 6 estimated lines); empty regions collapse into a neighbour; bled regions reach the slide edge; a lone long list flows into up to three columns (`listColumns: "auto"`, or a list payload's new `columns`). `paginate` continues on the same layout and pins moved blocks to their region.
    - **Composition output:** `SlideComposition.regions` (replaces `slots` for templates), `headingAreas`, `ComposedItem.region` and `ComposedItem.listColumns`, documented for the renderer and the PPTX exporter (RR-81).
    - **Schema:** the block `region` pin and the list `columns` field on content payloads; `Slide.layout: "auto"` (reserved; `opf format` drops it); `design.mirror`; a theme record's `design` (the six shared keys, between the deck's design and the layout's). `LayoutDiagnostic` gains `region-unknown`, `region-kind`, `region-full` and `layout-unplaced`; `DesignHintSource` gains `theme`.
    - **Validation:** new rules `opf/layout-template`, `opf/layout-region` (template errors; `composeSlide` refuses such a record), `opf/layout-removed` (a removed 0.18 id that resolves nowhere, with a safe fix), `opf/region-unknown`, `opf/region-kind`, `opf/region-full` and `opf/layout-unplaced` (warnings).
    - **Migration:** `spec/reference/layout-migration.json` maps the 278 ids of the 0.18 default catalog to the 28 built-ins and their design settings; `migrate()`, `migrateSlide()`, `convertLayoutRecord()`, `layoutMigrationRow()`, `removedLayoutRow()` and `LAYOUT_MIGRATION` are exported from the root. `layoutContent` and `layoutStructure` read a template's regions.
    - **Transition:** until the default catalog moves to `@openpresentation/gallery` 2.0.0 (RR-80, RR-83), 0.18 records (`opf-layout/v1`, `placeholders`, placeholder groups) still validate and compose exactly as in 0.18; they are removed, with no alias, before 0.19 is released.

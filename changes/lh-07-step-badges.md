LH-07: numbered step badges and a connector for process and agenda, and short lists as an arrowed flow. A layout region's new `sequence` key (`steps` or `numbered`) lists the steps of a sequence. In a `steps` region, two or more text or list blocks draw under numbered badges: an accent circle 1.75 times the step text across, its numeral at the step text size in the scheme color readable on the accent, on one connector line through the badge centers. A one-entry numbered list gives its badge its number and draws as a heading over its description. A lone list draws its entries beside badges down a connector, and a lone list of 3 to 6 entries of one or two words is a row of chips joined by arrows. In a `numbered` region, a numbered list draws beside badges. Steps keep the text size they have without the badges (a guarded rule, `step-badges`), and right-to-left decks run from the right. Core reports the drawing as `SlideComposition.steps`, list entries as `ListEntryLayout.badge` (`fitList` option `badges`) and adds `listNumberLabel`, `STEP_BADGE`, `STEP_BADGE_GAP` and the `STEP_FLOW_*` limits. The preview draws them, and the PPTX export writes native shapes: an ellipse holding its numeral, a line, rounded rectangles and `rightArrow` or `leftArrow` presets, each tagged `OPF_STEPS_V1`. Import consumes those shapes and gives a list its numbering back. The gallery's `process` layout uses `steps`; `agenda` and `agenda-split` use `numbered`.

The `folio` theme and its `emerald-ink` color scheme: green-black ink on a cool, neutral paper white with an emerald lead, deep plum for the contrasting series and ochre, petrol, sage and rose for the rest, set in the new `playfair-display-lora` pairing, for reports and narrative decks. It has the light, light alt, dark and dark alt variants of the other themes, and every text pair meets the palette checks (ink on paper 15.3:1).

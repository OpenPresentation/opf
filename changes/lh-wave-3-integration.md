LH wave 3 integration (output-changing where two wave 3 features meet): `composeSlide` holds every wave 3 rule that rearranges or resizes blocks (LH-89 nested gaps, LH-65 empty columns, LH-72 measure column, LH-78 quote lead size and card, LH-06 automatic band or pill, LH-68 scorecard tiles, LH-86 long-text rows, LH-66 minimum measure, LH-67 lead plus stack, LH-70 paragraph columns) to one size guard: a slide is composed once with all of them, and only when one changed something is it also composed without them, so that no block's fitted body size goes below what it is without the rules unless that leaves fewer findings (never below the readability floor), with no new finding, split word or paragraph under the minimum measure. LH-48's body growth, LH-49 and LH-83's recompositions and LH-80 and LH-49's tracks are composed with the rules that read well in them and kept only when they read as well. Where features meet: paragraph columns and a lead plus stack keep their own cells (LH-52 centers only the scored grid or LH-71's row, and LH-48 does not grow them); a quote beside another block in a centered split takes its own card in the middle of its cell (LH-78 with LH-52); long texts LH-86 moved into one region keep their rows; LH-06's band or pill takes precedence over LH-48's growth of the same blocks; a chart LH-71 stacks above a short takeaway with a plot wider than 3:1 takes LH-49's filling track. Line breaks of a text are reused across the compositions of a slide (per width measure), so the corpus composes in about 1.4 times wave 2's time.

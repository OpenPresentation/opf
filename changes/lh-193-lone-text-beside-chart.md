LH-193 (drawing change): a text alone beside the slide's one chart grows and is centered again, as `design.chartPrimary` drew it before 0.35. On an automatic slide whose `composition` is a weighted row or column (`mode: "row"` or `"column"` with two `weights`) of one chart and one text, the text (a block of its own, or the one block of a plain group) is the lone block of its cell (LH-48): it grows by the type scale's steps while it fills at most two thirds of the cell, a short message on to its display size, and sits in the middle of the cell. The same holds on a layout slide whose body is one chart and one text, the text alone in a single-block region (`max: 1`). The 0.34 example, a chart with "Revenue grew in three of four quarters." beside it, draws the text at 56 px again (0.35.0's replacement drew it at 28 to 32 px at the top of its side). A text that already fills half its cell keeps its size and place, nothing is set smaller, and `fill: false` turns it off. The `drawing` declaration names the built-in layouts whose slides draw differently: `two-column`, `comparison` and `three-column` have single-block regions, so a slide on them with only a chart and a text grows the text (and LH-170's note band under the chart keeps the size the note has without the band). No layout preview changes; every other slide draws as before.

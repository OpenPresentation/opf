LH-60, LH-56, LH-33 and LH-35 (output-changing for charts whose data does not fit their type, treemap and funnel labels, and sibling charts of one unit): charts draw every series or say which they leave out, labels inside marks are readable, sibling charts share a scale, and `opf/chart-color-only` checks lightness.
    - **Chart fallbacks (LH-60).** New `chartDrawPlan(chart, resolved, { language })` (core and `@openpresentation/opf/composition`) decides, from the data alone, how the preview and the PPTX export draw a chart: a `world` chart whose category labels are mostly not places (`isPlaceLabel`: CLDR region names in English and the deck language, region codes, US states, Canadian provinces, Australian states) is drawn as clustered columns of every series; a `box-and-whisker` chart with one value per category draws one box per series (the rows form one group named after the category column); a `pie` or `doughnut` of several series is a doughnut of nested rings, the first series innermost as PowerPoint orders them; a `scatter` whose X column holds no numbers plots its series against the category position. The PPTX export writes the same construct (the extra rings as further `c:ser` of the `c:doughnutChart`), reports it as a `chart-data-adapted` diagnostic with `adaptation: 'chart-fallback'`, and records the authored data so import restores it (a pie of several series imports as a doughnut). Charts in the examples move: every `world` chart over quarters draws columns, and multi-series pies and doughnuts draw rings.
    - **`opf/chart-series-dropped` (LH-60).** A new content warning (`chartDrawDiagnostics`) names the series a one-series construct (treemap, histogram and Pareto with categories, waterfall, funnel, a map of places, a single-series pie) does not draw, and a scatter X column without numbers.
    - **Labels inside marks (LH-56).** Treemap tile, funnel bar and map tile labels take core's `textColorForFill` of their fill (the full text colour at 4.5:1, else black or white), in the preview and, for the treemap (one `cx:dataLabel` per tile) and the funnel, in the PPTX export. Each tile's `cx:dataLabel` paragraph holds a text field of the label's content (`CATEGORYNAME` by default), because PowerPoint draws a point label whose paragraph holds no text as an empty label. PowerPoint draws funnel labels in its own contrast colour whatever the label text properties say (white on the default blue).
    - **Sibling charts share their axis (LH-33).** `composeSlide` gives the column, bar, line and area charts of one slide that run the same way and have one unit (the same number format of the first series, or the same series names) one value axis (`shareSiblingChartAxes`), which both engines draw.
    - **Colour never alone (LH-35).** `opf/chart-color-only` also reports neighbouring series (and neighbouring slices, the last beside the first) less than 11 CIE L* apart, the lightness step the engines keep, as a lightness pair.

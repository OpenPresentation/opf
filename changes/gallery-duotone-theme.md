The `duotone` theme and its `claret-ice` color scheme: two chromatic colors that ink each other (deep claret on a pale ice ground in the light variants, pale ice on claret in the dark ones), with raspberry and teal accents drawn from the same two hues, set in the new `bitter-source-sans-3` pairing. It has the light, light alt, dark and dark alt variants of the other themes, and every text pair meets the palette checks (claret on ice 15.3:1).

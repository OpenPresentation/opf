## Paginated group: 152 new entries, no existing entry moves

The coordinator accepted the overflowing slides as unpaginated composition: core reports `text-overflow` on each, and
`paginate` resolves it, as OPF handles overflow everywhere else. The proof is the `paginated` group
(`paginatedDecks()` in `test/layout-template-fixture.mjs`). It holds every sample slide that carries core's
`text-overflow`, put through core's `paginate`: 42 slides (a superset of the 10 that visibly overlapped) make 152 pages,
keyed `paginated <template> <blocks> <length> page <n>`. The 346 earlier entries are unchanged.

`test/layout-templates.mjs` asserts:

- each of the 42 source slides carries `text-overflow`, and the 10 that overlapped are among them;
- unpaginated, agenda 5 and 6 long and table-beside 5 and 6 long draw region elements outside their region box, so the
  check bites;
- every page continues on the source's layout and has no `text-overflow`, from core or from the renderer's `onDiagnostic`;
- every element drawn inside a `data-opf-region` group (7016 of them) lies inside that region's box, within 1.5 px. It reads
  `rect` and `image` boxes, `data-opf-box-*`, `data-opf-rich-lines`, text anchors, circles and lines; the region box
  already includes bleed;
- blocks drawn below the grid lie inside the slide (`section`, which has no body region).

All 152 pages were reviewed on the 7 review sheets: none overlaps, nothing is drawn into a title or past the slide edge,
and every page keeps its layout and repeats its title. Text that continues on the next page is split by core at a sentence
or word boundary, so some continuation pages start mid-sentence (timeline 4 long page 3, timeline 6 long page 2).

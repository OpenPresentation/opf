# SD-06: design.listBullet none, label layouts without bullets

The `faq` layout record sets `design.listBullet: "none"` (WI-6), so a FAQ draws its list entries without a marker and without the 1.1 em hanging indent: each entry starts at the left edge of its cell. `pillars` and `team` get the same hint, but the stability fixtures fill their regions with `text` blocks, so those two layouts draw the same pixels as before.

## What moved

- **Template baseline** (`layout-templates-rr96-color-variants.json`, `node test/layout-template-goldens.mjs --promote`): 26 of 485 slides, all `faq`: `faq 1-6 short`, `faq 1-6 long`, `feature faq described-10`, and the 14 pages of `paginated faq 4/5/6 long` (after WI-1's content sizing). `source.sha256` (the digest of the 28 records the fixture serializes) moves with the three records: `0f2637e8` to `3f0a627e`. Every other entry (459 slides, including every `pillars` and `team` slide) is unchanged.
- **Examples corpus** (`opf-examples-png.*`, the `golden.yml` workflow): `technical/slide-design-overrides.opf.json` gains a fifth slide (`#4`, a label list with `design.listBullet: "none"`), so the corpus input digest and one new slide entry change; no other slide moves.

## Review

The 28 template slides were reviewed on the review sheet from `--update`: the bullet glyph and the indent are gone, and the text is one hanging indent wider, which can move a line break; nothing else differs. The new example slide shows a 32 px label over a smaller description at the left edge, no marker.

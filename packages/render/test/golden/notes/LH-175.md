# LH-175: a Pareto chart of several series is drawn as clustered columns

Core's `chartDrawPlan` draws a Pareto chart of two or more numeric series as clustered columns of every series in data order
(`pareto-as-columns`), as the PPTX export already wrote it, so the preview and PowerPoint draw the same chart. The raster goldens of
the six example slides with a two-series Pareto chart move (each `Quarter, Current, Baseline`):

- `gallery/business-functions/partner-ecosystem-launch-kit#3`
- `gallery/design-and-media/visual-brand-system-sampler#3`
- `gallery/education/curriculum-approval-brief#7`
- `gallery/government/wildfire-evacuation-preparedness#3`
- `gallery/industries/consulting-margin-recovery-plan#3`
- `gallery/presentation-types/product-launch-narrative#7`

Reviewed on the before/after sheet linked in the PR. No other slide holds a Pareto chart of several series.

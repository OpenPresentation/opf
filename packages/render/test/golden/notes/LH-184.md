# LH-184: columns: "auto" keeps the largest text

`columns: "auto"` (and a region's `listColumns: "auto"`) now chooses among the allowed column counts by fitted size instead of always shrinking the largest count: the count with the largest shared size wins, fewer columns on a tie, and only a count in which no column overflows competes. When no count qualifies, the largest count is shrunk as before. A fixed count is unchanged.

One column replaces the columns only when its lines stay within the prose measure (`PROSE_MEASURE_CHARS`), or when it is at least a type-scale step (10%) larger. So a long list that went from 20 px in three columns to 24 px in one column now takes two columns at 23 px, and the paginated FAQ pages keep two columns at 27 px instead of one column of 90-character lines at 28 px.

The columns are balanced at the size they are fitted to, not the starting size (entries that wrap at the starting size can be one line at the fitted size): the 30-item bulleted agenda splits 15 and 15 and draws 23 px instead of 20 px.

# LH-184: columns: "auto" keeps the largest text

`columns: "auto"` (and a region's `listColumns: "auto"`) now chooses among the allowed column counts by fitted size instead of always shrinking the largest count: the count with the largest shared size wins, fewer columns on a tie, and only a count in which no column overflows competes. When the one column fits at a larger size than every count, the list stays one column. When no count qualifies, the largest count is shrunk as before. A fixed count is unchanged.

36 template-golden slides changed (agenda, list and faq with long entries, the 30-item bulleted agenda, and the paginated agenda, list and faq pages). Every changed slide fits, and no list is set smaller: the long agenda, list and faq slides take two columns instead of three at the same size, and the paginated faq pages draw 28 px instead of 27 px. "feature faq described-10" is unchanged (it keeps its three columns).

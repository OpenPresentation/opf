# LH-176: clustered bars as PowerPoint lays out a 150% gap

Core's `chartBarGeometry` reads the clustered gap width (150%) as a share of one bar, as PowerPoint does, so a category of `n` series holds
bars of `band / (n + 1.5)`; the preview read it as a share of the cluster (`band / 2.5 / n`) and drew two-series bars about a third thinner
than PowerPoint. A small chart's label fit uses the same slot, so two-series horizontal bar charts whose labels now fit drop their value
axis and label their bars (LH-03), in both engines. 21 example slides move, all with a clustered chart of two or more series:

- `gallery/business-functions/demand-generation-campaign-brief#3`, `identity-incident-executive-brief#3`, `investor-relations-update#7`, `onboarding-experience-redesign#3`
- `gallery/design-and-media/data-storytelling-atlas#7`
- `gallery/education/athletics-budget-review#3`, `online-program-launch-plan#3`, `school-safety-preparedness-brief#3`
- `gallery/government/recycling-participation-campaign#7`, `tax-portal-modernization#7`
- `gallery/industries/construction-safety-stand-down#7`, `grid-resilience-capital-plan#3`, `port-dwell-time-improvement-plan#3`
- `gallery/presentation-types/data-incident-postmortem#3`, `policy-briefing-for-advisors#3`, `seed-pitch-for-climate-risk-api#3`

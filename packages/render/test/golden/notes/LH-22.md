# LH-22 to LH-26: layout harvest round 1 (opf#942)

The layout-template goldens (`layout-templates-rr96-color-variants.json`) moved for 340 of 545 slides.

**Which slides.**

- Every carded fixture slide: the even block counts of each template, and the paginated pages that draw on cards. These follow three card rules:
  - A content card insets a sixteenth of its shorter side, from 12 to 24 px (`CARD_INSET`).
  - In a region anchored other than `top`, a card fills its cell and starts half a gap down (`CARD_FILL_SPACE`).
  - A quote, code or metric with no room inside the inset takes the whole card.
- Pillars, team, scorecard, two-column, three-column and hero at every count: their records changed (full-height cards, the new hero).
- Code panels: without host measurement, monospace text is measured at 0.6 em.

**How it was reviewed.**

- The candidate's review sheets (`artifacts/template-goldens/sheet-*.png`) were inspected.
  - Cards are roomier.
  - Rows of cards share one height and top edge.
  - Code wraps inside its panel.
  - No slide gained clipped or overlapping text.
- Main's own build reproduced the previous baseline byte-for-byte in the same environment (Windows, Node 26, bundled fonts), so the moved hashes come from the change and not the platform.

The example-corpus goldens were regenerated by the `golden` workflow on the branch (37 decks).

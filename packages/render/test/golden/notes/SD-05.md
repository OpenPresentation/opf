# SD-05: data readability (charts, tables, timelines, metric contrast)

Slide-design plan item WI-5 (P18, P19, P20, N2, P22; owner decision D3). It changes how every chart, table, narrow timeline and metric draws on a dark surface. Nothing else in the corpus moves.

## What moved

Of the corpus's 818 slides, **292 change** (526 keep their hash), measured with `OPF_GOLDEN_ARTIFACTS=changed node test/golden.mjs --update` against the default baseline (`opf-examples-png.fa-33-region-lists`) on macOS; the hashes match the pinned image (MR-31) and the `regenerate-goldens` workflow commits the baseline. Every changed slide contains a chart, a table, a timeline or a metric (the walk over each changed slide's payloads finds none without one). Grouped by cause:

| Cause | Slides | Layouts |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chart: no outer frame, wider value-axis intervals (at least 48 reference px, at most 8), 18 px text floor in a roomy box, series 1 lifted to 4.5:1 on a dark panel | 119 chart-only, 41 chart with a metric, 1 with a metric and a table | `chart` 100, `chart-beside` 21, `dashboard` 20, auto and custom compositions |
| Table: content-sized columns, whole words, horizontal rules only | 88 table-only, 40 with a metric, 1 with a chart and a metric | `table-beside` 80, auto compositions |
| Metric value lifted to 4.5:1 on a dark surface | 2 metric-only, plus every metric slide above on a dark background | `metrics`, `dashboard` |
| Timeline: narrow cells compose vertically | 1 (metric plus timeline) | the full-width timelines (the other timeline slides) keep their hash |

Per-slide before and after sheets for the gallery layouts, the peer-column cases and the P18 to P22 proposals, with the measured numbers (tick counts, contrast ratios, column widths, mid-word breaks), are in the pull request description.

## How it was checked

- Every changed slide was matched to its cause by walking the deck for `chart`, `table`, `timeline` and `metric` payloads (no changed slide has none; 292 = 119 + 88 + 40 + 41 + 1 + 2 + 1).
- The review sheet of the changed slides (`artifacts/golden/index.html` of the golden workflow) was read for the gallery cases: charts lose their frame and read at 0 to 100% in fives, tables keep every word whole, the three-column and two-column timelines stack vertically, the metric blue is lighter on the navy.
- Fixtures that hold exact bytes or digests of the same output were re-pinned with the reason in the test: `chart-finite-statistics`, `chart-table-data` (render), `chartex-fallback-main.json` and `rr-54-unchanged.json` (pptx); the 121 of 495 layout-template slides (`layout-templates-rr96-color-variants.json`) were promoted with `node test/layout-template-goldens.mjs --promote` after the candidate was reviewed.

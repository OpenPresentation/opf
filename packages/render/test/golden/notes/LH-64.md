# LH-64 and LH-04: card anchoring (opf#956)

The layout-template goldens moved for 118 of 538 slides: every carded fixture slide whose cards hold text, lists, quotes or metrics.

- **Top-anchored card rows (LH-04).** Cards hug the tallest card in their row instead of filling the cell.
- **Bottom- or middle-anchored rows of text-only cards (LH-64).** Cards hug their text and the row centres; cards of unequal content start their text at the top.
- **Unchanged.** Cards with a picture, metric, labelled item or group keep LH-22's full-height card with the content at the anchor.

**Known limitation.** In two-column with three or more blocks, the extra blocks sit in their own row, so hugged rows stand apart. That is the overfull case LH-46 (extra blocks join the grid) addresses.

**Review.** Review sheets were inspected; no text is clipped or overlapping. The example-corpus goldens are regenerated by the `golden` workflow on the branch.

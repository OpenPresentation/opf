# SH-18: the cycle layout and a fallback list that fills its region

The gallery gains a `cycle` layout (33 templates in all), so the layout-template goldens gain its samples: 26 new entries (cycle with 1 to 6 short and long blocks, and the paginated pages of its long samples, `paginated cycle 4 long` page 1 to 4, `5 long` page 1 to 4 and `6 long` page 1 to 6). The fixture hash changes because the gallery's layout list grew.

With one list block, `cycle` draws its arrangement (PowerPoint's Basic Cycle geometry where the instance fits the SmartArt domain, else OPF's own design); with several blocks the template's other regions draw as lists, as every template does with this fixture.

The shared fallback list of the pyramid, matrix and cycle arrangements now refits to fill its region and centers vertically, so ten existing entries changed, all long-content samples of those arrangements and none of any other layout: `pyramid 1 long`, `pyramid 5 long`, `matrix 1 long`, `matrix 5 long`, and pages 2 to 4 of `paginated pyramid 5 long` and `paginated matrix 5 long`. Short samples and the arrangements that draw their own design did not move. The six-block long samples carry the same dense-stress overlap as before in every template of this family; pagination, not the template, handles that content.

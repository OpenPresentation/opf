# LH-54, LH-55 and LH-79: dark surfaces

Core changes three colour roles; the renderer and the PPTX export draw what core resolves. No gallery record changes.

## Template baseline (`layout-templates-rr96-color-variants.json`)

`node test/layout-template-goldens.mjs --update`, reviewed, then `--promote`d: 346 of 545 slides moved; the source digest is unchanged. The fixture decks are the default `dark2` deck (`#011842`). A comparison of every moved slide's traced SVG with main's shows only these colour changes, and no geometry change:

- 291 carded slides: the card fill `#011842` (the background itself) becomes `#172C52` and its stroke `#4E4E4E` becomes `#334567` (LH-54).
- 24 metric slides: the same card, and the metric value is lifted against the card (`#308BC7` to `#489CD3`, 4.5:1 on the card).
- 31 code slides: the panel `#011842` becomes `#01112E` (the background at 70% lightness, LH-79), and the syntax colours are re-fitted to 4.5:1 on it (`#308BC7` to `#2E85BF`); 16 of them are also carded.

Main's own build reproduced the previous baseline byte-for-byte in the same environment (Windows, Node 26, bundled fonts), so the moved hashes come from the change and not the platform.

## Example corpus

A local `test/golden.mjs` run counts 63 slides in 52 decks; the `golden` workflow must regenerate them. Traced SVG against main:

- light decks with a code slide whose dark1 is near-black (`#0F172A`): the panel takes dark2 (`#334155`), and syntax colours are re-fitted (LH-79);
- dark decks with code: the panel is the background shaded (`#334155` to `#0A101D`);
- dark charts: series 2 and later reach 4.5:1 and series 2 keeps 20 L* from series 1 (`#3F6B88` to `#93BEE7` on the default navy, LH-55);
- no slide changes geometry.

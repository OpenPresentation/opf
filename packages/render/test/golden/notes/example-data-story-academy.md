# New example: Data Story Academy corporate programs

The examples corpus gains one hand-authored deck, `gallery/design-and-media/data-story-academy-corporate-programs.opf.json`
(16 slides), so the default baseline's source digest and deck count move (131 to 132 decks) and 16 new slide entries are
added. No existing deck changes, so no existing slide hash should move; a candidate that moves one is a regression.

The deck draws its panels, squiggles, cards and lockup from its own SVG assets placed as image backgrounds and promoted
regions on top of them. Review the 16 new slides in the golden workflow's review sheet against the macOS renders made
while adding the deck: the cover and closing contact bars keep the hand-drawn circle around "Book a call", the two charts
highlight the orange bar, and the testimonial and case-study cards keep their text inside the cards.

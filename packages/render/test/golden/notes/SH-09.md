# SH-09: the pyramid and matrix layouts

The gallery gains two layouts, `pyramid` and `matrix` (32 templates in all), so the layout-template goldens gain their samples: 52 new entries (pyramid and matrix with 1 to 6 short and long blocks, and the paginated pages of the long ones). No existing template slide changed; the fixture hash changes because the gallery's layout list grew.

With one list block, `pyramid` draws its arrangement (PowerPoint's Basic Pyramid geometry, or OPF's stepped pyramid when the entries do not fit), and `matrix` draws four quadrants for exactly four entries or cards for other counts. With several blocks the template's other regions draw as lists, as every template does with this fixture.

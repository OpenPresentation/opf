RR-81 (OPF 0.19 layout templates; needs core 0.19): the renderer draws template layouts (`opf-layout/v2`: `areas`, `rows`, `columns` and `regions`) from core's composed geometry. A list core lays out in columns (`item.listColumns`: a lone list in a region with `listColumns: "auto"`, or a list payload with `columns`) draws every column in its own box at one shared size, with numbering continuing across the columns; before, only the first column was drawn. Cards are core's `item.frameBox` and a region with `bleed` reaches the slide edge with no card, as core composes them. With `trace: true` everything an item of a region draws is grouped in `<g data-opf-region="<name>">`, and each list column is a group with `data-opf-list-column`, `data-opf-list-start`, `data-opf-list-end` and its `data-opf-box-*`. New SVG and PNG goldens cover the 28 built-in templates with 1 to 6 short and long blocks, lists in two and three columns, bled regions beside cards, mirrored templates, and the 152 pages core's `paginate` makes of the 42 sample slides that overflow (each page free of `text-overflow` and drawn inside its region boxes) (`test/layout-template-goldens.mjs`). Slides with an `opf-layout/v1` record or no layout draw exactly as before: no golden of the example corpus changes.

RR-17 (tests only; no package change): per-family host fixtures for the 35 open script, emoji and math families of the `scripts` pack (FF-44, FF-45). `test/script-family-hosts.mjs` (Node: `scripts: 'auto'` loads the family's package, every style resolves to the family itself as `exact`, a deck of samples of its script is drawn strictly in it at the face's own advances, and resvg paints it from its file) and `test/script-family-hosts-browser.mjs` (`npm run test:script-family-hosts-browser`: offline Chromium, the loader fetches exactly the family's pinned files, every run is drawn in a loaded FontFace of the family within 0.1 px of the accepted advance, right-to-left samples display right to left, the glyphs paint, the colour emoji face paints saturated pixels). The model is `test/script-family-fixture.mjs`; it writes `artifacts/script-family-hosts/{node,browser}.json` for the font tracker in core. A family that fails a check keeps failing it, strictly, as a recorded finding in `FINDINGS`: Noto Emoji (the monochrome raster stand-in) does not draw the emoji-presentation sequences (keycap, red heart, rainbow flag) in Chromium, which falls to another face at 1.000 em where the face gives 1.2695 em.

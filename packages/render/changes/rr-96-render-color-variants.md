RR-96 (output-changing for decks and slides that set `design.variant` or a picture or gradient `tone`; borders, gridlines, axis lines, chart text and the code panel change for every deck): every slide is drawn from core's resolved roles for its variant, as PowerPoint does with its colour map. `design.variant` (deck and slide, the `"dark1"` shorthand, the accent order) sets the background (bg1), text (tx1), surface (bg2), the series colour order and the neutrals; a variant hides the background of a less specific level. Table and chart frames, gridlines (tx1 at 15%), axis lines (25%), chart text (65%), the pie and treemap outlines, the video play icon, the code panel and its text, the metric neutral trend and the table header text come from the scheme-derived neutrals, so no fixed grey or white is left in the drawing code. A picture background that names its `tone` takes that tone's variant and a 40% scrim of the slide background (the renderer's own, core exposes none). Needs `@openpresentation/opf` 0.23.0.

RR-71 (breaking, needs core 0.18): logos live on the organization. The cover and section logo is core's `geometry.logo` (`shape`, `variant` and `reference` replace the removed `slot`) in the `onLight` or `onDark` artwork for the slide's background, and is named for assistive technology by its asset's alt text, else by the organization (`Acme Corp logo`); `design.logo: false` draws none. A zone logo is an ordinary image part (`image: "var:organization.logo.icon"`, or `var:organization.<id>.logo.icon` for a partner): the renderer no longer reads a `logo` furniture field, `LogoSet` or `design.logo` assets. Every furniture part is drawn from `part.box`, which core now lays out in a row (image, text, socials, date) with a gap between the parts, so a header or footer zone with more than one part moves; a zone with one part draws as before. A logo reference in drawn text (`var:organization.<id>.logo.icon`) no longer makes the preview load the faces for the organization's name.

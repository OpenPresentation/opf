RR-17 (output-changing in every export; opf-pptx#162): the vendored PPTX engine is pptxgenjs-plus 4.3.4, the maintained MIT fork of PptxGenJS (was PptxGenJS 4.0.1, unmaintained), still vendored byte for byte with its archive integrity, file hashes and npm provenance commit in `vendor/pptxgenjs/UPSTREAM.json`. Dependencies: `jszip` is replaced by `@node-projects/jszip` 4.3.0, plus `@rgrove/parse-xml` 4.2.3 and `pako` 3.0.2 (the browser bundle of `toPptx` + `fromPptx` grows by about 286 KB minified, 77 KB gzip). Measured on the 126 bundled examples and the 850 gallery documents, part by part ([docs/pptxgenjs-plus-migration.md](docs/pptxgenjs-plus-migration.md)): slide geometry, text, fonts, themes, tables, fields, notes text and embedded workbooks are unchanged; packages no longer contain ZIP directory entries; the `jpg` default content type is `image/jpeg`; chart categories are a flat `c:strRef`, the orphan series axis id is gone and pie/doughnut labels are hidden at the series level instead of per point; slide-to-chart relationships are relative; table frame ids and `p14:modId` values differ; `docProps/app.xml` counts paragraphs and notes. Three engine changes are undone in `src/vendor-compat.js` to keep the natively checked output: shapes without text get no empty text body, the notes master keeps its placeholders, and the notes master keeps a copy of the deck theme (FF-05). Fixed with it: a raster an `imageResolver` returns for an asset declared as SVG is embedded as that raster (the engine would add its broken-image placeholder as the SVG fallback), and the metric and image-placeholder alternative text is written correctly into self-closing shape properties.

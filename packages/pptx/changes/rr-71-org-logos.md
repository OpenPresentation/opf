RR-71 (breaking, needs core 0.18.0): logos live on the organization. The cover and section logo is the primary organization's `full` logo (or the one `design.logo` names, `var:organization.beta.logo`; `design.logo: false` draws none), picked per slide as the `onLight` or `onDark` asset of its shape, and its picture carries the organization path (`organization.0.logo.full.onDark`) in its tag and in `unresolved-asset` diagnostics. A header or footer `image` that is a logo reference (`var:organization.logo.icon`, a partner's `var:organization.beta.logo.wordmark`) is an ordinary furniture picture drawn at core's row-layout box; the slide manifest records the authored reference and the content key of the embedded bytes (`images`), and import returns the reference while the picture still embeds those bytes and the stored organization has a logo for it (a replaced, foreign or provenance-less picture imports as an image). `LogoSet` import and export and the zone `logo: true` flag are removed with no alias (the furniture manifest's `logos` key is gone), and `OPF_LOGO_V1` consumes the cover picture with the organization restored from the document record (an export without it gives the primary organization the picture as its one logo). In a zone that holds a logo beside the footer text, the text part is only as wide as its words (core's row layout): the slide's `ftr` placeholder keeps that box, and the master and layout placeholder spans the zone.

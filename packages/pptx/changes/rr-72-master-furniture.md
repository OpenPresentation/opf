RR-72 (output-changing for decks whose header or footer repeats a part PowerPoint has no placeholder for): such a part (a logo or other zone image, socials, header text, a second footer text or date) that is drawn the same on two or more slides is written once, on the slide master when every slide that shows such furniture draws it, else on a layout shared by the slides that do (one layout per distinct set: a logo with `onLight` and `onDark` assets gets a layout per background tone). A slide that hides it uses a layout with `showMasterSp="0"`. A part on one slide only, a part with a live field and a part that slide content covers stay on the slide; the last reports `furniture-on-slide`, because PowerPoint draws master and layout shapes beneath slide content. RR-11's `ftr`, `dt` and `sldNum` placeholders, the master and layout placeholders and `p:hf` are unchanged, so Insert > Header & Footer works as before. The lifted shapes keep their `OPF_FURNITURE_V1` tags (with the slot in place of the slide part) and each slide manifest lists them under `shared`, so `fromPptx` reads them from the slide's layout and master: a master logo returns as its `var:organization.logo.*` reference on every slide, a part deleted on the master is removed from every slide, and Hide Background Graphics on a slide removes the master parts from that slide. Importers before 0.18 ignore `shared` and read the rest of the furniture; shapes on a master or layout without OPF tags (a third-party template) are still not imported. Over the 127 core examples, 81 decks move furniture off their slides and every deck imports to the same document as before.

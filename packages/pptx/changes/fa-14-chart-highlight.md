FA-14 (needs the core that ships `chart.highlight`; a chart without `highlight` exports the same parts as before): chart emphasis exports natively. Series fills (`c:ser/c:spPr`, and `c:marker/c:spPr` on line, scatter and radar series) and per-point fills (`c:dPt`: `c:spPr` on a column or bar with a category highlight, a circular `c:marker` on a line, the slice fill on a pie or doughnut) carry the accent and muted colors core computes (`chartHighlightMarks`, `chartHighlightColors`), the same as the preview. The accent is `a:schemeClr accent1` when the deck theme holds the primary exactly, so re-theming in PowerPoint recolors it, and an sRGB literal otherwise; the muted color is an sRGB literal. Data labels inside a mark are written per point with the color that contrasts with that mark. Import does not infer a highlight from colors: in `full` provenance mode the authored `highlight` is recorded in the chart's `OPF_DATA_V1` record and restored while the cached data is unchanged (otherwise `chart-data-provenance-changed` says it was not). The chartex constructs are not highlighted and report `chart-option-adapted`.

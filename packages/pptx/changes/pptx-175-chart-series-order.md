opf-pptx#175 (output-changing for line, radar and scatter charts): classic chart parts follow the ECMA-376 `dml-chart.xsd` sequences. Line series write `c:marker` before `c:dLbls` (`CT_LineSer`: idx, order, tx, spPr, marker, dPt, dLbls, trendline, errBars, cat, val, smooth, extLst), radar series no longer carry the bar-only `c:invertIfNegative` (`CT_RadarSer`), and the scatter X `c:valAx` no longer carries the category-axis-only `c:auto`, `c:lblAlgn` and `c:noMultiLvlLbl` (`CT_ValAx`). pptxgenjs-plus 4.3.4 writes them that way; a last pass over each chart part (`src/chart-order.js`) puts every classic series (bar, line, area, radar, scatter, pie, doughnut) in its `CT_*Ser` sequence, and a series already in sequence keeps its bytes. Column, bar, area, pie and doughnut parts are unchanged. `test/chart-series-order.mjs` checks every classic chart type, with data labels and a category highlight, against the sequences (`test/helpers/chart-schema-order.mjs` now covers the area, radar, scatter, pie and doughnut groups, their series and the data point). The six `test/fixtures/rr-54-unchanged.json` entries with a line, radar or scatter chart are repinned; only `ppt/charts/chart1.xml` changed in each.

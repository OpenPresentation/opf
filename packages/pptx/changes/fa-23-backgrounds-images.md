FA-23 (OPF 0.15, breaking, output-changing for every deck with a picture): backgrounds and image blocks replace `design.slideImage`, `design.imageFill` and the slide image tags.
    - An image background (`{type: "image", src, alt, fit, focus, opacity, recolor, overlay}` or an image source string; core `backgroundImage`) exports without alt text as the native slide background fill: `stretch`, `tile` at the picture's intrinsic size from the top-left corner (no longer min(w, h)/4 cells), `cover` cropped around `focus` with `a:srcRect`, `contain` fitted with `a:fillRect` insets, `recolor` as `a:grayscl`/`a:duotone` then opacity as `a:alphaModFix`. With alt text (its own only) it is a full-slide picture at the back of the slide (`OPF background slides.N`) carrying `descr`, over the colour scheme's default background colour. The overlay is one shape directly above the picture, the whole slide or an edge band.
    - Every content picture is an image block (core `item.image`): fit and focus through core's `fitImage` (`cover` is now the default; `design.imageFit` replaces `design.imageFill`), and the treatments with 0.14's slide image DrawingML (`a:prstGeom` with core's guides, `a:ln` with a miter join, `a:grayscl`/`a:duotone` then `a:alphaModFix`), the overlay directly above. A placed block (`placement`) is drawn at its band in core's item order (after the headings, above the watermark, as the preview draws it); the 15 gallery image treatments keep 0.14's frames and per-picture paint order (picture, line, overlay) where 0.15 can express them (`test/image-treatments-014.mjs` lists the five that cannot).
    - `OPF_IMAGE_V1` and `OPF_IMAGE_OVERLAY_V1` tag image block pictures (with their own fields) and their overlays, the background picture and the background overlay; import reads them back as image blocks with their treatments and placement and as the slide's image background with its alt text, fit, focus, opacity, recolor and overlay. An edited picture becomes an ordinary image (`invalid-image-provenance`). Import also reads a native background picture fill as an image background, now including `stretch`, the focus of an off-center crop and a grayscale or duotone recolor. `OPF_SLIDE_IMAGE_V1`, `OPF_SLIDE_IMAGE_OVERLAY_V1`, `invalid-slide-image-provenance` and the slide image import are removed.

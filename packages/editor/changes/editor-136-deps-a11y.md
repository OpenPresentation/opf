Accessible names contain their visible text (WCAG 2.5.3 Label in Name; axe-core 4.14 enables `label-content-name-mismatch`). The slide navigator and sorter cards, the section toggles, the review panel's go-to buttons and the find panel's results no longer carry an `aria-label` that differs from what they show: a card is named by its number, title and "Hidden" badge, a section toggle by its name, a finding by its severity, message and location and a find result by its place and matching line. The slide position ("Slide 2 of 6", "selected"), a section's slide count and "Go to it." are their descriptions. The data grid's "Insert row above" and "Insert row below" buttons say "Insert 3 rows above" in their text when three rows are selected, the review panel's "Show again" button is named "Show again, <rule> findings", and a canvas editing target is named "Edit <field>: " followed by every word it draws, no longer cut at 80 characters. Also updates `@lezer/highlight` to 1.2.5 (a runtime dependency) and the development dependencies `pdfjs-dist` 6.4.299 and `axe-core` 4.14.0.

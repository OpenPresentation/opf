SH-01 of the OPF shapes work: the preset geometry engine that SH-02 (shape block), SH-04 (overlay shapes, connectors) and SH-06 (editor handles) build on. No schema key is added; SH-02 owns the format.

## What it adds

- `spec/reference/preset-geometry.json`: the 187 DrawingML preset shapes (ECMA-376 Part 1, `ST_ShapeType`) in the standard's own names and units (avLst, gdLst, ahLst with ranges, cxnLst, text rect, pathLst with w/h, fill, stroke and every path command), with `preset-geometry.schema.json`. Generated by `scripts/build-preset-geometry.mjs` from the standard's `presetShapeDefinitions.xml`; the source is downloaded, never committed.
- `@openpresentation/opf/shape-geometry` (new edge-safe subpath; `src/shape-geometry.ts`, data compiled in by `generate.mjs` like the symbol-font tables, kept off the root like them):
  - `PRESET_SHAPES`, `isPresetShape`
  - `presetAdjustments(name, width?, height?)` → `{ [guide]: { default, min, max, unit } }` in OPF units: fractions for the standard's 1/100000 values, degrees for the angle adjusts (the ones a polar handle moves); `null` for unbounded ranges
  - `presetGeometry(name, width, height, adjust?)` → `{ paths: [{ d, fill, stroke, commands }], textRect, connectionSites: [{ x, y, angle }], handles }`
  - `customGeometry(path, width, height)` for `path` (SVG path data in a 0 to 1 box, or `{ d, viewBox }`)
  - `adjustToDrawingML` / `adjustFromDrawingML` (the `a:avLst` integers), `evaluateGuideFormula`, `flattenShapePath`
- The full guide formula language and built-in guides; `arcTo` with the visual-angle semantics PowerPoint uses; paths with their own w/h evaluated in that space and scaled.

## Verified against PowerPoint

Every preset written as native `a:prstGeom` at two sizes (360x200, 150x260) and two adjust sets, filled and stroked (1 px), exported by PowerPoint 365 via COM at 1280x720 and compared with the engine's SVG: all 187 match. No stroked outline differs by more than 1 px; filled outlines differ by more than 1 px only at a few antialiased pixels of hairline tips (at most 13 px in a 640x360 cell: star32, rightBrace, leftBrace, star24, bracePair, flowChartOnlineStorage). Custom outlines written as `a:custGeom` from `customGeometry`'s commands: no pixel off by more than 1 px.

Found on the way:
- PowerPoint refuses to open a file whose avLst names a hexagon's or star's `adj` without its `hf`/`vf` factors, so `adjustToDrawingML` writes the whole avLst once any value is given.
- Two errata of the standard's file, corrected to what PowerPoint does: pie's text rect (t and r swapped) and curvedLeftArrow's tip site (`cd3`).
- Stroke joins of very acute tips differ between PowerPoint and SVG at thicker lines (join rendering, not geometry; for SH-02/SH-03).
